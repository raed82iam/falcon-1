Foundation architectural resolution update for FCR-0004 under the now-authorized Stage 5 WP-05 workstream

The Foundation workstream accepts the underlying problem: an admitted protection Application requires a governed, attributable, fail-closed way to target another admitted Application without direct coupling or Application-specific Foundation logic.

**Generic governed cross-Application route eligibility and isolation boundary**

For any admitted FIL message whose communication has already passed WP-04 message admission, Foundation shall be able to determine, without interpreting Application business payload meaning, whether exactly one declared and currently eligible route exists from the attributable producer/Application/Manifest context to the declared intended consumer/recipient context.

The WP-05 portion of this requirement shall provide only:
- attributable producer and consumer/Application identities;
- binding to the exact accepted WP-04 admission decision and declared message type;
- declared route identity and deterministic route-selection eligibility;
- explicit route state and isolation state;
- target/recipient scope consistency;
- authority/admission preservation without creating new authority;
- expiry/deadline eligibility where already governed by accepted message/admission metadata;
- fail-closed rejection for absent, undeclared, ambiguous, isolated, disabled, stale, mismatched, or otherwise ineligible routes;
- deterministic and reconstructable routing/isolation decision evidence;
- Application-neutral behavior with no FSATS/Guardian special case.

WP-05 SHALL NOT claim message delivery, acknowledgement, retry, queueing, backpressure, flow control, business execution, or successful protection effect. Those are separate later owners, primarily WP-06 and later work where applicable.
