Foundation architectural resolution update for FCR-0009 under the now-authorized Stage 5 WP-05 workstream

The Foundation workstream accepts the underlying problem: latency-sensitive cross-Application traffic must not lose governed deadline/expiry intent or bypass isolation and authority controls merely because it is performance-sensitive.

Foundation will not implement a trading Fast-Track special route, and WP-05 will not implement QoS transport execution. The problem is reformulated as a generic Foundation rule:

**Routing eligibility may consider only already-governed technical metadata that materially affects whether a route is currently eligible.**

Within WP-05 this may include:
- preservation of accepted message expiry/deadline metadata as opaque technical routing inputs;
- rejection when the deterministic routing observation point is already beyond a governed expiry/deadline that makes routing ineligible;
- route isolation/disabled-state enforcement;
- prohibition on treating producer-declared priority/QoS as business authority or as self-granted Foundation criticality;
- deterministic evidence showing which governed metadata affected route eligibility.

WP-05 SHALL NOT implement or claim: queues, scheduling, congestion policy, backpressure, flow control, bandwidth reservation, service levels, priority execution, latency guarantees, tail-latency measurement, deadline propagation through delivery hops, degradation execution, or delivery outcome evidence. Those belong to WP-06/resource-governance/observability or other separately authorized owners.

Accordingly:

`FOUNDATION_PROBLEM = VALID`
`WP05_RELEVANCE = MINIMAL_AND_BOUNDED`
`WP05_DISPOSITION = EXPIRY_DEADLINE_ROUTE_ELIGIBILITY_ONLY_WHERE_ALREADY_GOVERNED`
`QOS_TRANSPORT_EXECUTION = OUT_OF_SCOPE_WP05`
`APPLICATION_SELF_DECLARED_CRITICALITY = PROHIBITED`
`FCR_CLOSURE = NOT_YET_ELIGIBLE`

FCR-0009 remains open. WP-05 preserves only the routing-eligibility boundary needed for future QoS-aware transport; it does not satisfy or close the requested transport/QoS capability.
