Foundation architectural resolution update for FCR-0006 under the now-authorized Stage 5 WP-05 workstream

The Foundation workstream accepts the underlying problem: cross-Application evidence/event traffic must remain attributable, isolated, reconstructable, and protected from unsafe live/replay mixing.

Foundation will not implement event/replay delivery inside WP-05. The problem is split by architectural ownership.

**WP-05-owned generic routing portion**

WP-05 may ensure that an already-admitted message is routed only through an explicitly declared, attributable, eligible and non-isolated route whose producer, consumer, recipient scope and message-type binding match the accepted communication context. If accepted metadata distinguishes replay/test/non-authoritative traffic from authoritative operational traffic, routing eligibility may preserve that distinction and fail closed on route-context mismatch.

WP-05 may also preserve message identity, correlation/causation references and route decision evidence as opaque routing inputs/evidence. It shall not reinterpret them as event truth.

**Not WP-05-owned**

The following remain outside WP-05: event publication/subscription semantics, event-truth ownership, replay delivery, ordering guarantees, duplicate/correction execution semantics, evidence retention/journaling, delivery acknowledgement, retries, or any action-authority recreation from replay. These require their later authorized owners, including WP-06/WP-07 where applicable.

Accordingly:

`FOUNDATION_PROBLEM = VALID`
`WP05_RELEVANCE = LIMITED_BUT_REAL`
`WP05_DISPOSITION = ROUTE_CONTEXT_ISOLATION_AND_ATTRIBUTION_ONLY`
`EVENT_TRUTH_OR_PUBLICATION = OUT_OF_SCOPE_WP05`
`REPLAY_DELIVERY = OUT_OF_SCOPE_WP05`
