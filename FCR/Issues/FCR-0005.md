Foundation architectural resolution update for FCR-0005 under the now-authorized Stage 5 WP-05 workstream

The Foundation workstream accepts the underlying problem: an operational data-producing Application requires a governed, attributable, isolated cross-Application path to authorized consumer Applications without direct coupling.

Foundation will not implement a market-data-specific route. The problem is reformulated as the following generic Foundation requirement:

**Generic governed producer-to-consumer route eligibility for operational Application traffic**

For an admitted FIL message, Foundation shall be able to determine whether the producer/consumer relationship is declared, attributable, compatible with the accepted communication declaration, and mapped to exactly one eligible non-isolated route, without interpreting the payload as market data or any other business domain.

The WP-05 portion may implement only:
- exact producer/Application and intended-consumer binding;
- exact admitted message / message-type binding;
- declared route identity and route eligibility;
- recipient-scope consistency;
- isolation/disabled state enforcement;
- deterministic rejection for missing, ambiguous, mismatched, isolated, or undeclared routes;
- deterministic routing evidence and Application neutrality.

The following requested behaviors are not owned by WP-05 and remain deferred to their separately authorized owners: actual delivery, freshness enforcement beyond already-governed expiry eligibility, duplicate-effect handling, degradation signaling during transport, acknowledgements, retries, queueing, backpressure, flow control, or delivery outcome semantics.
