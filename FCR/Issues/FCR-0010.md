This response supplies Application-side semantics for Stage 6 planning only. It does not grant implementation authority and does not modify frozen P0-J semantics.

### 1. FSATS consumers of Foundation resource signals

At the Foundation boundary, resource truth is consumed by the exact admitted **Application principal**, not directly by an internal LSA/CSA/component.

- `Falcon Self-Aware Trading Application` consumes its own allocation, ceiling, pressure, enforcement, rebalance/restoration and request-decision truth. It projects bounded internal views to the operational branches that need them for Application-owned load shedding.
- `FSAPMA` consumes its own corresponding resource truth for provider/data acquisition workloads and internally projects only what its own branches require.
- `Falcon Trading Guardian Application` consumes its own resource truth and may also consume the governed outcome of an emergency protection request it originated for an exact affected Trading Application/scope, without becoming owner of that beneficiary Application's allocation.
- `FSTSimA` consumes only its own non-Live admitted resource truth when such runtime use is later authorized.
- Shared Web / Communication are independent Applications and must consume their own Foundation resource truth if applicable.
- The FSATS container/grouping identity is not a resource principal and there is no shared hidden FSATS allocation pool.

### 2. Application-level versus internal projections

Foundation signals must terminate at the admitted Application boundary.

Internal LSAs/components may receive an **Application-internal projection** derived from that exact Application's current Foundation truth, scoped to the minimum information needed for local scheduling/load shedding. Such an internal projection:
- is not a new Foundation decision;
- cannot expand the allocation/ceiling;
- cannot expose another Application's allocation;
- must remain traceable to the Foundation evidence/decision identity and observation time;
- must become stale/invalid when the underlying Foundation truth expires, is revoked or is superseded.

### 3. Required resource-signal semantics and fields

For allocation/ceiling/pressure/enforcement/rebalance/restoration projections and decisions, Application requires at minimum:
- exact Application identity;
- resource class and exact scope;
- granted/current allocation and ceiling as applicable;
- pressure/enforcement state;
- reserve/elevated-capacity state only where authorized and safe to expose;
- request/decision identity when derived from a request;
- authority/evidence identity;
- observation time and effective time;
- freshness/expiry or supersession semantics;
- correlation/causation identities;
- restoration/rebalance/revocation conditions where applicable;
- deterministic reason/category for denial, cap, reduction or revocation where safe to expose;
- version/epoch identity sufficient to reject stale replacement/substitution;
- fail-closed behavior for malformed, DENY-used-as-GRANT, future, expired, stale, mismatched, unauthoritative or cross-Application-substituted truth.

An Application must never infer additional capacity from absence of a pressure signal. `REQUESTED_RESOURCE != GRANTED_RESOURCE`.

### 4. Trading degradation hierarchy

The accepted Application business degradation order is contextually:
1. protect Guardian/protection work;
2. preserve reconciliation and authoritative truth recovery;
3. preserve open-position management;
4. preserve required protective/execution-critical and valid near-dispatch work;
5. active watch;
6. candidate evaluation;
7. discovery/enrichment;
8. research/learning/background improvement.

Typical shedding proceeds in the opposite direction: pause research/learning and new-strategy evaluation first, then reduce discovery/enrichment/candidate breadth and noncritical refresh/UI/reporting before protection-critical work.

The Application shall not shed Guardian protection, Unified Risk for existing exposure, open-position management, required protective execution, reconciliation, mandatory authority checks or evidence required to reconstruct material actions merely to preserve throughput.

Emergency escalation through the governed Application boundary is required when actual admitted capacity is insufficient to maintain the minimum safe protection/truth set after permitted local shedding/restriction.

### 5. Application reaction to Foundation decisions

- `REDUCE / LOWER CEILING`: immediately treat the new governed limit as authoritative, stop admission of lower-priority work as needed, shed/degrade according to the hierarchy, preserve protection/truth, and escalate only if minimum safe protection cannot be maintained.
- `DENY REQUEST`: do not assume capacity exists; continue within current grant, shed/restrict as necessary, and preserve the denial evidence. Guardian may tighten protection state if the denied capacity was necessary for safe protection.
- `TEMPORARY/PARTIAL GRANT`: consume only the exact granted quantity/scope and only during the effective period; do not treat a requested or preferred amount as granted. Temporary capacity must not become a permanent baseline by historical use.
- `REVOKE / REDUCE TEMPORARY GRANT`: stop relying on the revoked/reduced portion by its effective boundary, shed/restrict safely, preserve truth/evidence, and escalate only through the governed request path if needed.
- `RESTORE / INCREASE`: recovery is staged and evidence-based. A restored allocation alone does not automatically restore candidate breadth/new exposure if Guardian, Risk, data, broker, reconciliation or other recovery gates remain unsatisfied.

### 6. Cross-Application priority / reclaim rule

There is **no Owner-approved blanket rule** giving Trading/FSATS permanent Foundation-wide priority over Accounting, Warehouse or future Applications.

Foundation may reclaim or rebalance reclaimable allocations only under its own explicit governed Resource Governance policy and authority. Trading may provide evidence that an exact protection-critical workload has urgent safety impact, but Trading cannot self-declare Foundation criticality, seize another Application's allocation, or convert business importance into permanent platform priority.

Any future rule that grants a class of Applications or workloads higher Foundation-wide priority must be explicit, attributable, reviewable and separately Owner/governance approved. It must not arise implicitly from this FCR.

## Owner controlling resource-governance clarification ΓÇö Trading pressure/load-shedding through TARC

This comment supersedes the older Trading Application resource-consumer/request-role semantics in comment `5227022522` where they conflict with the following Owner-controlled design.

### 1. Trading resource priority remains resource-only

Trading-related Applications are the highest **Application-level priority domain for Foundation-governed technical resources only**. Foundation survival/protection/control floors and non-reclaimable Foundation reserves remain protected above all Application workloads.

This does not elevate Trading in governance, security, lifecycle, data truth, Risk, execution, awareness jurisdiction, or Owner authority.

### 2. TARC owns Trading Application internal resource governance

For the `Falcon Self-Aware Trading Application`, all Foundation allocation/ceiling/pressure/enforcement/rebalance/restoration/resource-decision truth terminates at the admitted Trading Application boundary and is managed internally by:

`Trading Application Resource Controller (TARC)`

TARC is the sole Trading Application role authorized to:
- maintain the Trading Application's internal resource state;
- distribute only the granted Trading allocation among Trading branches/LSAs/components/workloads;
- apply internal reservations, throttling, bounded concurrency, priority policy, load shedding, rebalance and staged restoration;
- aggregate internal resource needs and evidence;
- communicate resource requests and resource-governance responses with Foundation on behalf of the Trading Application.

Trading MSA/LSA/CSA/components, Risk, Execution, strategies, analysis roles and Guardian-related protection signals may supply evidence/need/urgency to TARC, but SHALL NOT become independent Foundation resource principals for Trading Application resources.

`TRADING_RESOURCE_CONTROL_ROLE = TARC`
`TRADING_FOUNDATION_RESOURCE_REQUEST_ROLE = TARC_ONLY`
`INTERNAL_RESOURCE_CONSUMERS_DIRECT_FOUNDATION_REQUEST = PROHIBITED`

### 3. No Guardian break-glass requester

Guardian may signal protection urgency and resource insufficiency to TARC. It does not directly request Trading Application resources from Foundation, including during crisis.

If TARC is unavailable/compromised/unable to preserve the minimum safe resource set, the condition fails closed and is handled through Foundation protection/resource governance rather than creating a second Trading requester identity.

`GUARDIAN_DIRECT_RESOURCE_ESCALATION_REQUEST = PROHIBITED`
`SECOND_TRADING_RESOURCE_REQUEST_PRINCIPAL = PROHIBITED`

### 4. Resource-pressure reaction model

TARC shall treat Foundation grant/ceiling/pressure truth as authoritative and shall not infer unused or extra capacity from silence or local observation.

TARC performs internal shedding/rebalance first, preserving the accepted protection/survival workloads as far as technically possible. When the current Trading grant cannot safely satisfy the required minimum, TARC submits the attributable evidenced request to Foundation. Foundation retains the final grant/cap/deny/reduce/revoke/rebalance/restore authority.
