### 1. Application-level principals allowed to communicate resource requests

- `Falcon Trading Guardian Application`: may submit **emergency/protection resource-escalation requests** for an exact affected Trading Application/scope when protection-critical work is threatened. Guardian does not self-allocate resources and does not become Resource Manager.
- `Falcon Self-Aware Trading Application`: may submit **ordinary evidenced capacity requests for itself** through its admitted Application boundary when its own workload requires additional capacity.

### 2. Internal evidence originators

LSAs, CSAs and components may originate evidence and recommendations **inside their owning Application only**. They are not direct Foundation resource principals.

The owning Application boundary must convert validated internal evidence into an attributable Application-level request. Required provenance should preserve the originating internal evidence identity without promoting that internal role into a Foundation principal.

### 3. Required message families

Application requires generic Application-neutral equivalents of these semantic families:

1. `RESOURCE_CAPACITY_REQUEST` ΓÇö ordinary Application request.
2. `RESOURCE_EMERGENCY_ESCALATION_REQUEST` ΓÇö protection/emergency request, including Guardian-originated requests.
3. `RESOURCE_PRESSURE_ALLOCATION_PROJECTION` ΓÇö current attributable Application allocation/ceiling/pressure/enforcement projection.
4. `RESOURCE_DECISION` ΓÇö grant / partial-grant / cap / deny with effective scope and conditions.
5. `RESOURCE_REBALANCE_RESTORATION_NOTICE` ΓÇö changed allocation and restoration/rebalance state.
6. `RESOURCE_REVOCATION_REDUCTION_NOTICE` ΓÇö withdrawal/reduction of previously available or temporary capacity.

Foundation may choose different technical contract names, but the externally observable semantics must remain distinguishable and reconstructable.

### 4. Required message semantics/fields

As applicable to each family, preserve at minimum:
- exact requesting Application identity;
- requester role/purpose classification;
- affected/beneficiary Application and exact scope;
- resource class(es);
- current admitted allocation/ceiling where relevant;
- minimum useful quantity/capacity and preferred quantity where meaningful;
- urgency/severity/priority basis;
- bounded requested duration and expiry;
- attributable evidence identities and evidence timestamp/version;
- correlation and causation identities;
- request/decision identity and version;
- decision result: GRANT / PARTIAL / CAP / DENY / REVOKE / REDUCE / RESTORE as applicable;
- effective-from / effective-until or equivalent governed lifetime;
- restoration/rebalance conditions;
- denial/cap/revocation reason category where safe to expose;
- observation/freshness time for pressure/allocation truth;
- explicit fail-closed behavior for malformed, stale, future-dated, expired, mismatched, unauthoritative or cross-Application-substituted data.

`REQUESTED_RESOURCE != GRANTED_RESOURCE` remains mandatory.

### 5. Trading degradation / protection hierarchy Foundation must preserve without interpreting business semantics

Application-side business order, contextually scoped, is:
1. Guardian/protection work;
2. reconciliation and authoritative truth recovery;
3. open-position management;
4. required protective/execution-critical work and near-dispatch work where still valid;
5. active watch;
6. candidate evaluation;
7. discovery/enrichment;
8. research/learning/background improvement.

Within an admitted Application allocation, lower classes shed first. The following must not be silently shed merely to preserve throughput: Guardian protection, Unified Risk for existing exposure, open-position management, required protective execution, reconciliation, required authority checks and evidence required to reconstruct material actions.

If actual capacity is insufficient to maintain safe protection, the Application must restrict/degrade and, where warranted, Guardian may submit an emergency escalation request rather than fabricate normal operation.

This hierarchy is **Application business evidence**, not self-granted Foundation technical criticality. Foundation owns the final technical resource priority/allocation decision.

### 6. Cross-Application priority rule

There is **no Owner-approved blanket rule** that FSATS/Trading as a whole always outranks future Applications such as Accounting, Warehouse or any other Application.

Trading business priority inside Trading does not mint Foundation-wide priority. Foundation should make cross-Application technical priority/resource decisions from explicit governed policy, attributable current evidence, safety/criticality context and admitted authority.

A genuinely protection-critical Trading request may provide evidence justifying elevated treatment for its exact scope and duration, but this remains a Foundation decision and does not create a permanent Trading entitlement or an authority to consume/reclaim another Application's allocation.

## Owner controlling resource-governance clarification ΓÇö TARC sole Trading resource-control/request role

This comment supersedes the older Application-side resource-request principal statements in comment `5227020571` for the **Falcon Self-Aware Trading Application** resource-governance scope.

### 1. Resource-priority scope

The accepted Owner rule remains **resource-only**:

- Trading-related Applications are the highest **Application-level priority domain for Foundation-governed technical resources**.
- This priority applies only to resource allocation/ceiling/grant/reduction/revocation/reclamation/rebalance/restoration/pressure/crisis resource decisions.
- Foundation survival/protection/control floors, non-reclaimable Foundation reserves, and Foundation resource-governance capacity remain protected above Application workloads.
- This resource priority does not create higher Trading authority in governance, security, data truth, Risk, execution, lifecycle, awareness jurisdiction, or Owner decision rights.

### 2. TARC is the sole Trading Application resource-control/request role

For the `Falcon Self-Aware Trading Application`, the sole internal authority responsible for Application resource governance and the sole role authorized to communicate with Foundation **about Trading Application resources** is:

`Trading Application Resource Controller (TARC)`

At the Foundation boundary, the admitted Application identity remains the `Falcon Self-Aware Trading Application`; `TARC` is the exact authorized requester/controller role inside that Application.

All Trading LSAs, CSAs, components, Risk, Execution, strategies, market-analysis roles, MSA, and Guardian-related signals may provide resource need/pressure/evidence to TARC, but they SHALL NOT independently submit Trading Application resource requests to Foundation.

TARC SHALL:
- consume the Trading Application's attributable Foundation allocation/ceiling/pressure/decision truth;
- maintain the Trading Application internal resource picture;
- distribute only the admitted Trading allocation internally;
- perform internal reservation/rebalance/throttling/load-shedding/restoration within the granted ceiling;
- aggregate resource need and evidence;
- submit ordinary or emergency Trading resource requests to Foundation when internal redistribution cannot safely satisfy the need;
- preserve `REQUESTED_RESOURCE != GRANTED_RESOURCE` and obey Foundation grant/cap/deny/reduce/revoke/restore outcomes.

### 3. No Guardian direct resource-request bypass

The previously contemplated direct Guardian-to-Foundation resource-escalation path is **not part of the controlling design** for Trading Application resources.

Guardian may detect a protection/resource emergency and provide protection evidence/urgency to TARC, but it does not become a resource requester or Resource Manager. If TARC becomes unavailable, compromised, or unable to maintain the minimum safe set, that condition is itself a fail-closed protection/resource-governance condition for Foundation handling; it does not mint a second Trading resource-request principal.

`TRADING_RESOURCE_REQUEST_AUTHORITY = TARC_ONLY`
`GUARDIAN_DIRECT_RESOURCE_REQUEST = PROHIBITED`
`TRADING_LSA_CSA_COMPONENT_DIRECT_RESOURCE_REQUEST = PROHIBITED`

### 4. Non-resource development/governance remains outside TARC

TARC is resource-governance only. It has no authority over Application development, self-improvement, architecture change, strategy evolution, Risk-model evolution, or other non-resource governance.

Those matters remain under the accepted Application self-awareness/governance chain: internal CSA/LSA evidence as applicable, **MSA as the final Application-level assessment/recommendation**, and **FSA for Foundation/OS governance, constitutional, authority, isolation and cross-boundary review**, subject to Owner authority.

`TARC_NON_RESOURCE_DEVELOPMENT_AUTHORITY = NONE`
`APPLICATION_DEVELOPMENT_GOVERNANCE = MSA_WITH_FSA_UNDER_ACCEPTED_GOVERNANCE`

### 5. Scope boundary

This clarification makes TARC the sole resource-control/request role for the **Falcon Self-Aware Trading Application**. It does not silently make TARC the resource authority for separate admitted Applications such as FSAPMA, FSTSimA, Trading Guardian, Shared Web or Communication; any resource-controller/requester role for those independent Applications must remain separately attributable and governed.
