OWNER REQUIREMENT ADDENDUM ΓÇö BACKUP/ROLLBACK PRECONDITION AND OWNER ROLLBACK ORDER ΓÇö 2026-08-18

The Project Owner has added a mandatory condition to the standing pre-approval concept:

`AUTO_ACCEPT_ELIGIBLE -> GOVERNED_BACKUP_OR_ROLLBACK_PLAN_REQUIRED`

No proposal may be auto-accepted under a standing Owner pre-approval policy unless the exact proposal carries a current, attributable, validated backup/rollback plan appropriate to the affected scope.

Foundation clarification is requested for the authority/evidence side of this addition:

1. Define the generic requirements that make a backup/rollback plan eligible to satisfy a standing pre-approval policy, including immutable plan identity/version, exact pre-change baseline/snapshot or recoverable state identity, affected scope, validation evidence, rollback preconditions, recovery constraints, expected rollback outcome, expiry/supersession and evidence references.
2. Confirm fail-closed behavior when the backup/rollback plan is missing, stale, superseded, incomplete, unverifiable, incompatible with the exact proposal, or unable to restore the affected governed state.
3. Define how the standing policy must bind the accepted proposal to the exact backup/rollback plan identity effective at auto-accept time.
4. Define evidence that must be retained for each policy-derived auto-accept: proposal identity, policy/version, backup/rollback plan identity, pre-change state identity, match decision, acceptance outcome and resulting state.
5. Define the generic Owner rollback-request/rollback-order authority boundary. Shared Web Command Center shall allow the Owner to submit an attributable rollback order/request for an already accepted/auto-accepted change, but `UI_ROLLBACK_CLICK != ROLLBACK_AUTHORIZATION != ROLLBACK_EXECUTION != ROLLBACK_COMPLETION`.
6. Clarify whether rollback itself requires a fresh authority decision, step-up authentication, target/application admission and current safety/readiness checks, and how a rollback request is rejected if rollback would be unsafe or no longer valid.
7. Define required result/evidence states so Command Center can display `ROLLBACK_REQUESTED`, `ROLLBACK_ACCEPTED`, `ROLLBACK_REJECTED`, `ROLLBACK_IN_PROGRESS`, `ROLLBACK_COMPLETED`, `ROLLBACK_FAILED` or authoritative equivalents without inventing state.
8. Preserve that rollback cannot silently restore superseded authority, credentials, trust, deployment authority, Live trading authority, Kill release/revival authority, or other separately governed privileges.

Additional mandatory boundaries:

```text
AUTO_ACCEPT != EXECUTION_AUTHORITY
AUTO_ACCEPT_WITH_BACKUP_PLAN != GUARANTEED_ROLLBACK_SUCCESS
BACKUP_EXISTS != BACKUP_VALID_FOR_THIS_CHANGE
ROLLBACK_REQUEST != ROLLBACK_AUTHORIZATION
ROLLBACK_AUTHORIZATION != ROLLBACK_EXECUTION
ROLLBACK_EXECUTION != AUTHORITY_RESTORATION
ROLLBACK_COMPLETED != TRUST_RESTORED_UNLESS_SEPARATELY_VERIFIED
```

Shared Web remains policy-management, presentation and request-transport only. It does not become rollback authority or recovery authority.

2. Shared Web Command Center is the Owner-facing policy evaluation surface. It shall evaluate the proposal against the CURRENT Owner standing approval policy/list and any current Owner-authored policy comments/conditions/evidence that form the governed policy record.
3. Only after exact policy match and authority validation may Shared Web emit an Owner-derived auto-accept request/decision artifact attributable to the Owner's standing authorization.
4. `APPLICATION_SELF_DECLARED_AUTO_ACCEPT = FORBIDDEN`.
5. `AI_SELF_DECLARED_AUTO_ACCEPT = FORBIDDEN`.
6. `PRODUCER_CLAIMED_POLICY_MATCH != OWNER_DERIVED_POLICY_MATCH`.
7. The proposal producer may provide matching facts, but cannot be the authority that decides the match is sufficient.
8. Rollback is symmetrical: the Application/AI may provide a rollback capability/plan and may report rollback readiness/results, but may not initiate or authorize Owner rollback by itself.

