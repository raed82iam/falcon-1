## 1. Purpose

This audit evaluates whether Falcon Foundation’s documentary baseline is coherent, complete within its declared scope, subordinate to Vision and Constitution, internally traceable, and safe to close before any preparation or implementation begins.

It does not evaluate software because no Falcon implementation exists.

## 2. Audit Result

The audit result is:

```text
PASS
```

No unresolved material defect was found in Falcon’s Foundation concept, authority model, security model, Self-Awareness limits, Contract model, verification design, platform strategy, or implementation boundary.

The Roadmap and Readiness Report status refreshes are complete under GOV-046.

## 3. Vision and Constitution

The audit confirms:

- Vision is the highest authority;
- Constitution remains subordinate only to Vision;
- Protect Capital, Manage Capital, then Grow Capital remains the Prime Objective;
- capital protection prevails over opportunity;
- lower documents cannot redefine Falcon’s identity;
- no technical choice has been placed above enduring principles;
- autonomy remains bounded by authority, evidence, and safety; and
- financial authority is absent from Foundation.

**Result:** `PASS`.

## 4. Falcon Identity and Scope

Falcon is consistently defined as a Self-Aware Autonomous Financial Operating System.

It is not defined as:

- a trading bot;
- an Expert Advisor;
- a single strategy;
- an AI application;
- a broker connector;
- a prediction engine;
- or a vendor-specific platform.

Trading, intelligence, analytics, automation, risk, prediction, and future capabilities remain subordinate means.

**Result:** `PASS`.

## 5. Document Authority

- Plans;
- Reports;
- Authority Instruments;
- implementation;

- canonical source;

- Self-Awareness document-governance limits.

The audit found no lower document claiming power to override Vision or Constitution.

**Result:** `PASS`.

## 6. Jurisdiction and Authority

The audit confirms:

- jurisdiction precedes authority;
- delegation cannot create jurisdiction;
- authority is exact, scoped, attributable, time-bounded, revocable, and challengeable;
- possession, capability, identity, signature, message, reference, or access does not create authority;
- candidate subjects cannot activate themselves;
- validity, Acceptance, reliance, Activation, implementation, operation, production, and financial authority remain distinct; and
- current Project Owner authority concentration is explicitly recorded.

**Result:** `PASS`.

## 7. Guardian and Capital Protection

Guardian remains:

- protective rather than profit-seeking;
- independent from opportunity pressure;
- capable of restriction and Safe Mode;
- unable to be bypassed by lower authority;
- subject to evidence and challenge;
- unable to restore unrestricted authority without competent independent confirmation; and
- aligned with capital survival over missed opportunity.

**Result:** `PASS`.

## 8. Self-Awareness

Self-Awareness is correctly bounded as system self-knowledge rather than human consciousness.

It governs awareness of:

- state;
- health;
- component fitness;
- data quality;
- decision quality;
- confidence;
- uncertainty;
- knowledge limits;
- authority;
- drift;
- dependencies;
- and when to stop, restrict, learn, or refuse.

Self-Awareness cannot create truth, authority, jurisdiction, Acceptance, or Activation by itself.

**Result:** `PASS`.

## 9. Self-Maintenance and Evolution

The Foundation supports future:

- diagnosis;
- repair;
- update;
- replacement;
- optimization;
- migration;
- and controlled evolution.

Every change remains:

```text
Observation
→ Diagnosis
→ Proposal
→ Authority
→ Candidate
→ Independent Verification
→ Completeness
→ Scoped Validity
→ Acceptance
→ Exact Activation
→ Monitoring
→ Rollback
```

No autonomous change may alter Vision, Constitution, jurisdiction, governing policy, Guardian boundaries, or financial authority.

**Result:** `PASS`.

## 10. Modularity and Replaceability

The audit confirms:

- Adapter isolation;
- no external-library leakage across layers;
- no vendor lock-in;
- no persistence-technology assumption;
- no transport assumption;
- communication-order independence unless contractually guaranteed;

- provider-neutral security and custody;
- replaceable build and automation providers; and

**Result:** `PASS`.

- Data Platform;

## 12. Decision and Intelligence

The audit confirms:

- predictions are Claims, not truth;

- AI output remains attributable, scoped, challengeable, and non-sovereign; and
- deterministic and judgment-based evaluation remain distinct.

**Result:** `PASS`.
