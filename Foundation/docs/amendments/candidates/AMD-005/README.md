## Purpose

AMD-005 separates technical Foundation protection from Application business protection.

It proposes:

- **Falcon Foundation Guardian (FFG):** protects Falcon’s technical body, isolates technical harm, and governs Platform Safe Mode;
- **Falcon Self-Awareness System (FSA):** observes, correlates, diagnoses, verifies, and performs bounded repair under approved authority;
- **Application Guardian:** a reserved future role that may protect capital and Application-domain interests without transferring business meaning into Foundation.

## Governing Statement

> FSA understands technical condition. FFG imposes bounded emergency protection. The competent Foundation mechanisms execute the directive.

> FFG protects the platform. A separately governed Application Guardian may protect the business performed above the platform.
