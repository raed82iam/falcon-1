# AMD-005 Owner Approval Package

**Status:** Approved by GOV-060  
**Stage 1:** Blocked

## 1. Decision Requested

Approve the architectural separation of:

- Falcon Foundation Guardian as technical Foundation protection authority;
- Falcon Self-Awareness System as awareness, diagnosis, verification, and bounded repair capability; and
- a reserved future Application Guardian as business and capital protection authority.

## 2. Proposed Approval Set

1. AMD-005 v0.1.
2. ADR-I010.
3. AUT-002 v2.0 as the proposed successor to AUT-002 v1.0.
4. Guardian Authority and Knowledge Matrix.
5. Registry and Migration Change Set.
6. VPL-GDN-001 as a verification plan only.
7. Architectural Impact Assessment.

## 3. Decisions Preserved for Later

Approval SHALL NOT by itself:

- activate or implement FFG;
- create an Application Guardian;
- execute VPL-GDN-001;
- alter an active restriction;
- start Stage 1;
- authorize code, deployment, production, cloud operation, financial connection, or financial activity;
- approve technical criticality values, trigger thresholds, survival set, high availability, stop-channel realization, release matrix, or irreversible-action quorum.

## 4. Supersession Control

AUT-002 v1.0 SHALL remain Approved and effective until:

1. the Owner explicitly approves AUT-002 v2.0;
2. affected Contracts, registries, baselines, and cross-references are prepared and accepted;
3. a controlled documentary activation record is approved; and
4. historical preservation is verified.

Approval of the concept is not silent activation.

## 5. Required Owner Decisions

The Owner is requested to decide:

1. approve or reject ADR-I010;
2. approve or reject AUT-002 v2.0;
3. confirm the FSA/FFG separation;
4. confirm Foundation/Application Guardian separation;
5. authorize preparation of supporting ADRs, catalogs, Contracts, and successor verification documents;
6. preserve the prohibition on Stage 1 until a separate explicit instruction.

## 6. Suggested Approval Statement

> أنا، رائد عموره، بصفتي Project Owner والسلطة الدستورية الحالية لمشروع Falcon، أوافق على AMD-005 v0.1 وADR-I010 وAUT-002 v2.0 ووثائق الدعم التابعة لها، وأعتمد الفصل المعماري بين FSA وFalcon Foundation Guardian، وبين Foundation Guardian وApplication Guardian المستقبلي. أفوّض إعداد تحديثات التحكم الوثائقي والقرارات والمواصفات والعقود وخطط التحقق التابعة للمراجعة فقط. لا يمنح هذا الاعتماد أي صلاحية لبدء Stage 1، أو تنفيذ أو تفعيل Guardian، أو إنشاء Application Guardian، أو تنفيذ خطط التحقق، أو كتابة كود، أو النشر المحلي أو السحابي، أو الإنتاج، أو الربط أو النشاط المالي، وكل ذلك يحتاج إلى تفويض مستقل وصريح.

The Project Owner approval is recorded by GOV-060. The suggested statement remains historical proposal text and is not the controlling approval declaration.
