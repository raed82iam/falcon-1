# AMD-005 Constitutional Compliance Report

**Status:** Acknowledged and Approved  
**Approval Record:** GOV-060  
**Scope:** Proposed Guardian boundary only

## 1. Vision Alignment

AMD-005 preserves Falcon’s Prime Objective:

1. Protect Capital.
2. Manage Capital.
3. Grow Capital.

It recognizes that capital protection depends on a technically trustworthy Foundation while preventing Foundation from assuming financial or Application-domain knowledge it does not own.

## 2. Authority Alignment

The proposal:

- derives FFG authority from Approved governance and AUT-001;
- prohibits self-created jurisdiction and self-expansion;
- binds emergency authority to declared condition, scope, consequence, and duration;
- separates protection, execution, verification, release, and repair;
- preserves independent challenge and oversight; and
- requires stricter evidence for material release decisions.

It therefore supports the constitutional principles of bounded authority, least authority, separation of duties, attribution, reversibility, and independent verification.

## 3. Capital Protection Alignment

Separating FFG from a future Application Guardian does not remove capital protection.

It places:

- technical platform protection with FFG; and
- capital, exposure, and business protection with a future competent Application/domain authority.

The two may coordinate through an authorized minimal technical request without transferring business meaning into Foundation.

## 4. Self-Awareness Alignment

AMD-005 is compatible with proposed AMD-004 because:

- FSA supplies awareness, diagnosis, verification, and bounded repair;
- FFG supplies restriction and Platform Safe Mode;
- neither may approve its own material challenge;
- FSA cannot release FFG restrictions; and
- FFG cannot alter FSA or own Self-Evolution.

## 5. Safety and Continuity Alignment

The proposal requires:

- proportionate containment;
- preservation of unaffected safe operation;
- fail-protective behavior under severe uncertainty;
- persistent restrictions;
- protected evidence;
- controlled recovery;
- independent stop and compromise handling; and
- no automatic return to normal.

## 6. Historical and Governance Alignment

AMD-005 does not overwrite `AUT-002 v1.0`, accepted ADRs, or active baselines. It proposes a versioned successor and a controlled migration path.

No Stage 1, implementation, testing, activation, deployment, cloud, production, or financial authority is granted.

## 7. Result

`CONSTITUTIONALLY_ALIGNED_AS_A_CANDIDATE`

This result supports Owner review. It is not an approval, activation, or operational authorization.
