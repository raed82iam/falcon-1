# CON-031 — Protection Audit and Evidence

**Identifier:** CON-031 — Proposed Reservation  
**Version:** Proposed 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed

Every Guardian registration, request, validation, decision, provisional action, directive, execution attempt/result, restriction-state change, recovery observation, release decision, compromise event, stop-channel use, failover, and challenge SHALL produce CON-008/SEC-002-conforming evidence.

Records SHALL preserve identities, authorities, technical trigger, evidence/contradiction, confidence/uncertainty, scope, requested and selected action, narrower/stronger reason, owner, actual effect, failures, Platform/domain modes, persistence, release conditions/authority, final disposition, provenance, integrity, and lineage.

No producer, FSA, FFG, Application Guardian, executor, collector, signer, or challenged authority may alone declare a material case complete or conclusively valid.
