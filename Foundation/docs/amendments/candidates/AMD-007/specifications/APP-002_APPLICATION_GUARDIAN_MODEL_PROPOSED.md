# APP-002 — Application Guardian Model

**Identifier:** APP-002 — Proposed Reservation  
**Version:** Proposed 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Stage 1 Authority:** Not Granted

## Purpose

Define the generic Application Guardian role used by any Application domain without Foundation redesign.

## Requirements

- an Application Guardian SHALL reside outside Foundation and remain bounded to its Suite/domain;
- it MAY understand domain meaning authorized by its Suite;
- it SHALL be registered through CON-024 and request Foundation protection through CON-022;
- it MAY restrict its own domain within mandate;
- it MAY request investigation, resource protection, isolation, Platform Containment, or Platform Safe Mode;
- it SHALL NOT directly control another Application, Foundation, or Platform mode;
- it SHALL NOT acquire authority from knowledge, urgency, installation, or request possession;
- required Guardians SHALL be independently deployable from any single protected Application where technically possible;
- compromise, absence, or uncertainty SHALL trigger the Suite’s declared fail-safe policy;
- Foundation receives technical summaries only.

Trading Guardian conforms through RSK-006. Future Accounting, Medical, Inventory, or Transportation Guardians require their own domain Specifications but no Foundation change.
