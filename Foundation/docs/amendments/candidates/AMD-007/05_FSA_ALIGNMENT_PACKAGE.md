# FSA Alignment Package

**Status:** Proposed

## Verified Boundaries

FSA:

- understands Foundation integrity, operation, FIL, Service Bus, information flow, Runtime, Lifecycle, Resources, Persistence, documentation, configuration, security, authority compliance, dependencies, propagation, recovery, conformance, performance, and capability gaps;
- restores only previously Approved trusted Foundation state under Approved Repair Playbooks;
- may create/test isolated non-authoritative candidates under separately Approved authority;
- produces candidate-specific Owner Approval Packages;
- cannot interpret or modify Application business meaning/state;
- cannot approve, activate, deploy, or trust its own candidate;
- cannot release FFG restrictions or change FFG mandate.

## Owner Workflow

Every candidate package SHALL bind one candidate version and one Evidence Set and include weakness, evidence period, root cause, modifications, behavior delta, benefits, measurements, uncertainty, dependencies, Application technical impact, conformance, security/authority/compatibility assessments, Sandbox/Digital City/independent results, deployment/canary/monitoring/rollback/corrective plans, recommendation, and exact decision.

Silence is not approval.

Owner decisions SHALL distinguish approve for canary, approve for production, approve with conditions, request changes/evidence, defer, and reject. Approval does not execute deployment.

## Required Corrections

- activate AWR-001 v2.0 through a coordinated documentary decision;
- remove “final Guardian architecture” from unresolved matters after GOV-062 activation alignment;
- create Repair Playbook Contract/catalog;
- create candidate environment and Owner Center realization documents;
- define post-adoption verification and rollback Contracts.

## Result

FSA architecture is aligned. Its execution capabilities remain intentionally unavailable.
