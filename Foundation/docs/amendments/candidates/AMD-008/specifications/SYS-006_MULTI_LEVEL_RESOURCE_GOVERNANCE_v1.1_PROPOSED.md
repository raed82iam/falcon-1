# SYS-006 — Multi-Level Resource Governance

**Identifier:** SYS-006  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Status:** APPROVED PENDING COORDINATED ACTIVATION  
**Approval Record:** GOV-063  
**Activation:** Not Authorized

## Foundation Resource Governance

Foundation owns total-resource truth, protection floors, recovery reserves, Application allocations, quotas, ceilings, technical priority classes, pressure handling, and governed redistribution.

Every allocation decision SHALL identify Application, purpose, resources, limits, duration, priority basis, authority, evidence, and restoration conditions.

## Application Resource Governance

Each Application MAY distribute only its admitted allocation among its MSA, branches, LSAs, components, execution functions, and internal services.

An Application SHALL NOT:

- exceed or reinterpret Foundation grants;
- consume another Application's allocation;
- convert business preference into Foundation technical criticality;
- conceal internal pressure or dependency failure;
- self-approve additional Foundation resources.

Applications MAY request additional resources with evidence. Foundation MAY rebalance according to approved priority, technical criticality, current system condition, availability, isolation, and survival policies.

## Failure and Uncertainty

Unavailable or uncertain enforcement SHALL reduce authority and admission. Pressure handling SHALL preserve Foundation survival floors, shared-service integrity, Application isolation, evidence, and progressive restoration.
