## Validation Obligations

Before Stage 1, approved plans must prove:

1. zero-Application Foundation completeness;
2. Trading and Accounting Application coexistence without Foundation redesign;
3. install/update/suspend/recover/remove isolation;
4. forbidden direct cross-Application access;
5. quota and redistribution enforcement;
6. Application failure containment;
7. all four origin-aware production-bound paths, without artificial lower-tier insertion;
8. FSA rejection of business-quality evaluation;
9. MSA rejection of Foundation-governance authority;
10. self-development cannot expand ownership or authority;
11. service ownership has no duplicate authoritative responsibility;
12. dependency failure and recovery order are reconstructable.
13. FSA outcomes cannot be used as documentary activation, implementation, deployment, or production-adoption authority.

Plan approval does not authorize execution.
