# AWR-007 — Local Self-Awareness

**Identifier:** AWR-007 — Proposed Reservation  
**Version:** 1.0 Candidate  
**Acronym:** LSA  
**Status:** Approved Design — Not Effective  
**Approval Record:** GOV-061  
**Owner:** Parent Application or Approved Operating Layer  
**Architectural Location:** One Falcon Application or Approved Operating Layer  
**Governing Authority:** Falcon Vision; Falcon Constitution; proposed ADR-I009  
**Implementation Authority:** Not Granted

## 1. Purpose

LSA maintains evidence-based awareness of one owning Application or Approved Operating Layer.

## 2. Scope

LSA understands:

- the parent Application’s purpose and responsibilities;
- internal operational and business-domain condition;
- capabilities and limitations;
- components and dependencies;
- performance, confidence, and evidence quality;
- failures, weaknesses, gaps, and improvement opportunities;
- owned user and business concepts where authorized by the Application;
- owned CSAs and their assessments;
- effects requiring MSA or FSA escalation.

## 3. Non-Scope

LSA SHALL NOT:

- own another Application’s awareness;
- acquire cross-Application or Foundation authority;
- claim FSA conformance;
- bypass MSA for ordinary ecosystem coordination;
- bypass FSA where Foundation conformance is required;
- approve its own authority expansion or protected promotion;
- modify Foundation components;
- reinterpret Foundation technical state as Application-owned truth.

## 4. Parent Application Ownership

The parent Application SHALL remain authoritative for:

- LSA identity and purpose;
- Application-owned business meaning;
- Application users, roles, accounts, and data;
- Application-specific fitness;
- LSA admission, lifecycle, and retirement subject to Falcon rules.

Foundation may protect, isolate, monitor, and technically host LSA without owning its business model.

## 5. Local Self Model

The Local Self Model SHALL:

- declare Application and LSA identity;
- distinguish facts, estimates, assumptions, interpretations, and unknowns;
- preserve source, evidence, time, freshness, confidence, and scope;
- identify capability and dependency state;
- identify business and technical limitations;
- represent owned CSA state without replacing CSA evidence;
- preserve history, challenge, correction, and supersession.

## 6. Escalation

LSA SHALL escalate:

- cross-Application dependency, conflict, capability, or incident to MSA;
- Foundation integrity, resource, security, FIL, Service Bus, persistence, isolation, or recovery impact to FSA through governed paths;
- protective threats to Guardian or Security through authorized paths;
- authority questions to Authority Engine or competent governance.

Escalation transfers information, not ownership.

## 7. CSA Coordination

LSA:

- determines whether a component may be proposed as CSA-eligible under Approved rules;
- receives CSA assessments;
- challenges unsupported CSA claims;
- aggregates component effects into Application awareness;
- prevents a CSA from self-approving change;
- routes cross-Application and Foundation-impact matters upward.

## 8. Application-Owned Development and Improvement

LSA MAY identify and propose changes within the owning Application’s declared scope.

Every proposed change SHALL preserve:

- Application ownership;
- independent verification and approval;
- MSA coordination where ecosystem impact exists;
- FSA conformance where admission into Falcon is requested;
- rollback or corrective-action obligations;
- evidence and historical integrity.

## 9. Normative Requirements

- **AWR-007-REQ-001:** Every first-class Application or Approved Operating Layer SHALL own one LSA or an explicitly Approved equivalent.
- **AWR-007-REQ-002:** LSA SHALL remain bounded to its parent.
- **AWR-007-REQ-003:** LSA SHALL own Application awareness but SHALL NOT acquire cross-Application or Foundation authority.
- **AWR-007-REQ-004:** LSA SHALL preserve source, evidence, confidence, uncertainty, history, and challenge.
- **AWR-007-REQ-005:** LSA SHALL escalate ecosystem matters to MSA and Foundation-impact matters to FSA.
- **AWR-007-REQ-006:** LSA SHALL coordinate owned CSAs without replacing their evidence.
- **AWR-007-REQ-007:** LSA SHALL not self-approve protected change or bypass FSA conformance.
- **AWR-007-REQ-008:** LSA SHALL keep Application user and business knowledge outside Foundation ownership.

## 10. Acceptance Evidence

Acceptance requires:

- one-Application ownership;
- correct handling of Application users and business concepts;
- CSA coordination;
- MSA and FSA escalation;
- rejection of cross-Application authority;
- independent change approval;
- historical reconstruction.

## 11. Unresolved Matters

- APP-001 relationship and mandatory LSA admission profile;
- Application fitness catalog;
- MSA summary contract;
- direct FSA escalation policy;
- LSA recovery and replacement profile.
