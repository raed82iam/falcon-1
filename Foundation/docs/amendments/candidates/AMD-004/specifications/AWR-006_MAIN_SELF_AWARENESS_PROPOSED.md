# AWR-006 — Main Self-Awareness

**Identifier:** AWR-006 — Proposed Reservation  
**Version:** 1.0 Candidate  
**Acronym:** MSA  
**Status:** Approved Design — Not Effective  
**Approval Record:** GOV-061  
**Owner:** Falcon Applications Awareness Authority — Proposed  
**Architectural Location:** Falcon Applications Environment  
**Governing Authority:** Falcon Vision; Falcon Constitution; proposed ADR-I009  
**Implementation Authority:** Not Granted

## 1. Purpose

MSA maintains evidence-based awareness of the Falcon Applications ecosystem as an ecosystem.

MSA understands the general purposes, condition, relationships, collective capabilities, dependencies, conflicts, incidents, readiness, and improvement needs of Falcon Applications.

## 2. Architectural Position

MSA is:

- outside Falcon Foundation;
- below FSA in awareness rank and conformance escalation;
- above LSAs for ecosystem aggregation and coordination;
- not a replacement for any LSA;
- not an authority inherited by Applications merely through reporting.

## 3. Scope

MSA governs awareness of:

- registered Falcon Applications and Approved Operating Layers;
- general declared Application purpose;
- Application availability, readiness, and declared health;
- cross-Application dependencies and conflicts;
- collective Application capabilities and gaps;
- shared Application incidents;
- cross-Application effects and propagation;
- ecosystem-level operational and general business context;
- ecosystem improvement opportunities and coordination;
- escalation to FSA where Foundation integrity or Falcon conformance is affected.

## 4. Non-Scope

MSA SHALL NOT:

- become a Foundation component;
- own Foundation Technical Fitness;
- replace FSA conformance review;
- replace an LSA’s authoritative Application awareness;
- own every Application user, record, account, portfolio, order, position, or decision;
- inspect private Application details without declared purpose and authority;
- grant cross-Application authority;
- approve its own implementation, promotion, or expansion;
- bypass Guardian, Authority Engine, Security, Risk, governance, or FSA.

## 5. Ecosystem Model

The MSA ecosystem model SHALL contain only the detail required for:

- dependency and capability understanding;
- collective readiness;
- cross-Application impact;
- conflict and incident coordination;
- improvement coordination;
- escalation and conformance preparation.

Every assertion SHALL identify the contributing LSA or authoritative source, scope, abstraction level, evidence, freshness, confidence, privacy classification, and known limitations.

## 6. Information Abstraction and Privacy

LSAs SHALL disclose the minimum information necessary.

MSA SHOULD receive:

- Application identity and version;
- declared purpose and capability summary;
- readiness and fitness summary;
- dependency and conflict summary;
- incident and impact category;
- evidence reference;
- privacy-preserving aggregate.

MSA SHALL NOT require customer identities, credentials, or raw business records unless a separately Approved rule establishes necessity, jurisdiction, protection, and minimization.

MSA possessing a summary does not make it owner of the underlying fact.

## 7. Collective Readiness

MSA MAY evaluate collective readiness for a declared Applications-ecosystem purpose.

Collective readiness:

- is not Foundation Technical Fitness;
- is not an individual Application’s full fitness;
- does not grant authority;
- shall expose missing, stale, contradictory, or incomplete LSA evidence;
- shall not conceal an unready critical Application behind a favorable aggregate.

## 8. Cross-Application Coordination

MSA MAY:

- identify missing collective capability;
- expose cross-Application dependency or conflict;
- coordinate investigation and improvement proposals;
- request LSA reassessment;
- escalate shared risk or Foundation impact;
- prepare evidence for FSA conformance.

MSA SHALL NOT command an Application outside valid authority or rewrite an LSA’s authoritative fact.

## 9. Relationships

- **FSA:** receives technical and conformance-relevant summaries; FSA does not absorb MSA business knowledge.
- **LSA:** authoritative source for one Application’s awareness; MSA aggregates and challenges but does not replace it.
- **CSA:** normally reaches MSA through its owning LSA; direct escalation requires Approved policy.
- **Guardian/Security/Authority Engine:** remain separate competent authorities.
- **Domain authorities:** retain business, financial, risk, and decision ownership.

## 10. Evidence and Provenance

MSA SHALL preserve:

- source LSA and Application;
- scope and abstraction;
- evidence reference and integrity;
- time, freshness, and confidence;
- aggregation and derivation rules;
- contradictions and missing contributors;
- challenges, corrections, and supersession;
- escalation and decision references.

## 11. Failure and Degraded Behavior

Missing or untrusted LSA evidence SHALL produce explicit uncertainty.

MSA failure SHALL:

- not erase LSA state;
- not grant favorable collective readiness;
- expose loss of ecosystem awareness to FSA and affected authorities;
- preserve safe independent Application behavior where permitted;
- prevent unsupported cross-Application decisions.

## 12. Normative Requirements

- **AWR-006-REQ-001:** MSA SHALL exist only in the Falcon Applications environment.
- **AWR-006-REQ-002:** MSA SHALL maintain one scoped Applications ecosystem model.
- **AWR-006-REQ-003:** MSA SHALL not replace an LSA or own its private Application truth.
- **AWR-006-REQ-004:** MSA SHALL use minimal governed summaries and preserve privacy.
- **AWR-006-REQ-005:** MSA SHALL expose collective dependency, conflict, capability, incident, and readiness conditions.
- **AWR-006-REQ-006:** MSA SHALL escalate Foundation-impact and conformance matters to FSA.
- **AWR-006-REQ-007:** MSA SHALL not bypass FSA conformance.
- **AWR-006-REQ-008:** MSA SHALL not acquire cross-Application authority from awareness rank.
- **AWR-006-REQ-009:** MSA assessments SHALL remain attributable, scoped, challengeable, and reconstructable.
- **AWR-006-REQ-010:** MSA SHALL not approve its own authority expansion or promotion.

## 13. Acceptance Evidence

Acceptance requires:

- aggregation from multiple LSAs without ownership transfer;
- privacy-minimized summaries;
- contradiction and missing-LSA handling;
- cross-Application dependency and conflict detection;
- Foundation-impact escalation to FSA;
- proof that MSA cannot bypass FSA or replace an LSA;
- reconstruction of collective readiness.

## 14. Unresolved Matters

- Applications-environment authority charter;
- ecosystem summary schema;
- direct CSA escalation exceptions;
- privacy and retention profiles;
- relationship to future APP-001.
