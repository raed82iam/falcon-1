# Constitutional and Authority Review

**Status:** Acknowledged and Approved  
**Approval Record:** GOV-062

## 1. Vision

Trading Guardian directly serves capital protection. FFG preserves the technical platform required to exercise that responsibility safely. Their separation prevents Foundation from becoming trading-specific while retaining Falcon’s financial purpose.

## 2. Constitution

The proposal preserves:

- capital protection before opportunity;
- bounded and attributable authority;
- separation of recommendation, decision, approval, and execution;
- truthful uncertainty;
- independent verification;
- safe-state and recovery discipline;
- immutable evidence; and
- Plug-and-Play replaceability without hidden authority.

## 3. Jurisdiction

| Authority | Jurisdiction |
|---|---|
| Trading Guardian | Trading Suite protection and Trading-domain restrictions |
| FFG | Foundation protection, Platform modes, and cross-Application technical isolation |
| FSA | Foundation awareness, diagnosis, verification, and bounded repair |
| AUT-001 | authority validation, delegation, restriction, and revocation state |
| execution owners | technical or broker-facing action they own |
| Project Owner/governance | unresolved policy, architecture, and high-consequence authority decisions |

Delegation SHALL NOT create jurisdiction.

## 4. Required Safeguards

- Trading Guardian cannot directly isolate another Application.
- FFG cannot inspect trading or other business payload meaning.
- FFG independently evaluates every request.
- Trading Guardian cannot become Broker Execution.
- FSA cannot decide Trading safety.
- `PLATFORM_NORMAL` and `TRADING_NORMAL` are independent.
- neither Guardian may conclusively validate its own material challenge or recovery.
- provisional containment requires pre-authorization, bounds, evidence, expiry or review, and independent reconstruction.

## 5. Authority Conflict

No direct constitutional conflict exists.

An unresolved governance dependency remains: exact consequence classes, maximum autonomous containment duration, irreversible-action quorum, technical-criticality authority, and release authority must be separately approved before activation.

## 6. Conclusion

`CONSTITUTIONALLY_ALIGNED_AS_PROPOSED`

This conclusion is an assessment, not approval or implementation authority.
