# Falcon Standards Registry

**Identifier:** STD-000  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Status:** Approved  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-24  
**Approval Record:** GOV-006
**Owner:** Falcon Standards Authority

## 1. Purpose

This registry controls the Standards that govern Falcon artifacts and recurring practices.

Standards define required discipline. They shall not define Falcon’s financial purpose or silently introduce system behavior.

## 3. Jurisdiction Rule

When a Standard needs required system behavior to be effective, that behavior shall be established in a Specification. The Standard may then govern the evidence, format, or practice used to demonstrate conformance.

## 4. Approval Rule

A Standard becomes binding only through explicit approval by its designated authority. Use by a team or tool does not constitute approval.
