# Progressive Autonomy Standard

**Identifier:** STD-010  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-24  
**Approval Record:** GOV-003
**Owner:** Falcon Standards Authority  
**Governing Authority:** Constitution Articles 26–30 and 36A–36C

## 1. Purpose

This Standard defines how autonomous authority is classified, granted, reduced, reviewed, and revoked.

## 2. Levels

| Level | Name | Permitted effect |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | Observe | Collect and report evidence without change |
| A1 | Recommend | Produce proposals without execution authority |
| A2 | Approved Repair | Execute a pre-approved, bounded maintenance playbook |
| A3 | Isolated Development | Construct and verify a candidate outside authoritative operation |
| A4 | Bounded Promotion | Promote a low-consequence candidate inside a valid Safe Evolution Envelope |
| A5 | Independent Approval Required | Execute only after independent high-consequence approval |
| A6 | Constitutionally Reserved | Autonomous execution prohibited |

## 3. Classification

Autonomy SHALL be assigned to an action class, not permanently to an actor.

Classification SHALL consider:

- maximum capital effect;
- authority changed or exercised;
- reversibility;
- system and dependency reach;
- security and data consequence;
- duration and persistence;
- uncertainty and evidence quality;
- ability to isolate and roll back; and
- impact on independent protection.

## 4. Required Record

Every autonomous grant SHALL identify:

- actor and owner;
- action class;
- autonomy level;
- purpose;
- scope and resources;
- required Fitness to Operate;
- constraints and stop conditions;
- evidence prerequisites;
- effective and expiry time;
- review authority; and
- revocation mechanism.

## 5. Rules

1. An actor SHALL operate at the lowest sufficient autonomy level.
2. A higher level SHALL NOT be inferred from successful lower-level operation.
3. Missing required fitness, trust, or evidence SHALL reduce authority.
4. A6 includes Vision amendment, constitutional amendment, self-authorization, weakening independent protection, and changing the rules governing autonomous approval.
5. A4 SHALL require a valid pre-approved Safe Evolution Envelope.
6. A5 approval SHALL be independent of the change producer and affected subject.
7. Every level SHALL remain attributable, observable, interruptible, and revocable as appropriate.
8. Expired authority SHALL NOT remain valid through continued execution.

## 6. Review Evidence

Approval requires:

- consequence analysis;
- authority source;
- demonstrated fitness;
- failure and abuse cases;
- stop and revocation tests;
- independence analysis;
- residual risk; and
- review date.
