# Constitutional Compliance Standard

**Identifier:** STD-004  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-24  
**Approval Record:** GOV-006  
**Owner:** Falcon Standards Authority  
**Governing Authority:** Falcon Vision; Falcon Constitution Articles 1–5 and 39–48
**Supersedes:** None  
**Superseded By:** None

## 1. Purpose

This Standard defines the evidence and review discipline required to determine whether a governed Falcon artifact, decision, capability, change, release, or exception complies with the Vision and Constitution.

Compliance review interprets higher authority within a declared case. It does not amend the Vision or Constitution.

## 2. Applicability

A constitutional compliance review SHALL occur before approval or promotion of:

- a new or materially changed Specification or Standard;
- an enduring architectural decision;
- a release baseline;
- a material authority delegation;
- an autonomous or self-generated change with material consequence;
- a capability capable of affecting protection, authority, trust, evidence, or capital;
- an exception to subordinate authority; and
- any matter reasonably suspected of constitutional conflict.

Review rigor SHALL increase with consequence, irreversibility, uncertainty, authority reach, and potential effect on capital or independent protection.

## 3. Required Compliance Record

Every review record SHALL identify:

1. subject identity, version, owner, and status;
2. review scope and decision requested;
3. applicable Vision provisions and constitutional Articles;
4. material facts and evidence;
5. affected authorities, duties, prohibitions, and protected interests;
6. plausible conflicts and alternative interpretations;
7. effect on Protect Capital, Manage Capital, and Grow Capital in their binding order;
8. effect on authority, accountability, explainability, security, resilience, and future choice;
9. unresolved uncertainty, omissions, and dependencies;
10. required containment, correction, conditions, or follow-up;
11. reviewer identities, competence, independence, and conflicts of interest;
12. decision, rationale, scope, and effective duration; and
13. evidence and decision-record references.

## 4. Review Method

The reviewer SHALL:

1. establish the authoritative document versions;
2. determine the substance and possible consequence of the subject;
3. identify all applicable higher-authority obligations;
4. test favorable and adverse interpretations;
5. determine whether a lower document creates contradiction, waiver, hidden amendment, or excessive authority;
6. assess whether protection, restraint, reversibility, evidence, and independent control are proportionate;
7. identify any conflict that requires containment;
8. record a decision using the vocabulary in Section 5; and
9. preserve the complete review record.

Silence, prior approval, technical success, profitability, urgency, or absence of observed harm SHALL NOT establish compliance.

## 5. Decision Vocabulary

- **Compliant:** Evidence supports compliance within the declared scope.
- **Compliant with Conditions:** Compliance depends on explicit, owned, verifiable conditions.
- **Non-Compliant:** A material conflict exists and the subject cannot proceed as proposed.
- **Inconclusive:** Evidence or authoritative interpretation is insufficient.
- **Not Applicable:** The reviewer demonstrates why no constitutional question exists within the declared scope.

Only `Compliant` or legitimately satisfied `Compliant with Conditions` may support approval.

## 6. Independence

The author or beneficiary of a material subject SHALL NOT be its sole constitutional reviewer.

High-consequence, self-evolution, capital-affecting, or broad-authority matters SHALL receive review independent of the proposer and affected operational authority.

Human participation does not substitute for evidence. Automated review may assist but SHALL NOT acquire constitutional amendment or final ratification authority.

## 7. Conflict and Stop Rule

A suspected material conflict SHALL be recorded and contained in proportion to possible harm.

A confirmed conflict SHALL result in rejection, suspension, isolation, correction, or termination. A lower authority SHALL NOT waive the conflict.

When evidence is inconclusive and possible harm is severe, review SHALL favor narrower authority, reversible action, delay, or non-action.

## 8. Exceptions and Interpretations

An exception record SHALL identify its subordinate rule, justification, scope, owner, risk, controls, approval, expiry, and review trigger.

No exception may authorize violation of the Vision, Constitution, or non-waivable protection.

An interpretation with enduring effect SHALL be recorded through constitutional governance. Repeated operational interpretation SHALL NOT become an informal amendment.

## 9. Traceability and Re-Review

The compliance decision SHALL trace to the exact subject and higher-authority versions reviewed.

Re-review is required when:

- the subject changes materially;
- the governing Vision or Constitution changes;
- a relied-upon fact, assumption, control, or authority changes;
- observed behavior contradicts the review;
- consequence or scope expands; or
- a condition or exception expires.

## 10. Prohibited Practices

Compliance review SHALL NOT:

- rely on labels instead of substance;
- quote isolated provisions to defeat higher purpose;
- conceal uncertainty or adverse evidence;
- treat a checklist as proof without analysis;
- approve first and construct justification afterward;
- allow the subject to approve its own exemption;
- convert temporary practice into constitutional meaning; or
- represent `Inconclusive` as approval.

## 11. Compatibility and Transition

Approval of this Standard SHALL NOT retroactively invalidate an approved artifact solely because its earlier review used a different form.

Existing approved artifacts SHALL enter review when materially changed, when relied upon for a new high-consequence decision, or when evidence indicates a possible constitutional conflict. A known substantive conflict receives immediate treatment regardless of age.

## 12. Acceptance Evidence

Conformance to this Standard requires:

- complete traceability to the Vision and Constitution;
- explicit adverse-case and conflict analysis;
- reviewer competence and independence evidence;
- recorded decision and conditions;
- containment of identified material conflicts;
- preserved version-bound evidence; and
- successful reconstruction by an authorized reviewer.
