## 3. Closure Finding

The closure finding is:

> **Falcon Foundation’s documentary meaning is complete for the declared Foundation phase, internally governed, aligned with Vision and Constitution, and ready to transition—through separate authority—to bounded Stage 0A preparation.**

## 4. Meaning of Closure

Closure means:

- the Foundation philosophy, identity, governance, specifications, Standards, ADRs, Contracts, Catalogs, Designs, Plans, verification definitions, traceability, platform direction, security boundaries, and Self-Awareness limits are complete for this phase;
- AMD-003 substantive work is closed;
- the final gap audit is accepted;
- remaining work is explicitly classified as execution, Activation, implementation, production, platform realization, cloud realization, financial-domain work, or later governance;
- the current Foundation baseline becomes the fixed governing starting point for the next phase; and
- future changes require governed amendment and impact review.

## 5. What Closure Does Not Mean

Closure does not mean:

- Falcon is implemented;
- tools are installed;
- a Windows environment exists;
- Linux is admitted;
- Oracle Cloud is provisioned;
- a Provider or Profile is active;
- the Pipeline or trace implementation exists;
- VPL or VPL-BST has executed;
- evidence completeness has been established;
- Stage 0A may begin without separate authority;
- Stage 1 is authorized;
- production is authorized;
- financial connectivity is authorized; or
- capital-bearing activity is permitted.

## 6. Preserved Execution State

At closure:

- Stage 0A is `NOT AUTHORIZED`;
- Stage 0B is `NOT AUTHORIZED`;
- Stage 0C is `NOT AUTHORIZED`;
- Stage 1 is `NOT AUTHORIZED`;
- production is `OUT OF SCOPE`;
- cloud deployment is `NOT AUTHORIZED`;
- financial activity is `PROHIBITED`;
- no Authority Instrument exists for execution;
- no Profile, Provider, Gate, Pipeline, runner, trace implementation, or environment is active; and
- no real data, credential, account, market connection, order, position, portfolio, or capital enters scope.

## 7. Next Phase

The next phase is:

```text
Stage 0A — Governed Preparation
```

It may be considered only through a separate proposal and Authority Instrument defining:

- exact scope;
- exact tools and sources;
- exact Windows preparation environment candidate;
- external bootstrap identity and time;
- evidence obligations;
- financial isolation;
- stop conditions;
- cleanup;
- duration;
- consequence limits; and
- explicit non-authorities.

Foundation closure creates eligibility to propose Stage 0A.

It does not authorize Stage 0A.

## 8. Change Protection

After closure:

- Vision remains highest authority;
- Constitution remains next;
- Foundation documents remain controlling within scope;
- changes require the document class and authority process defined by GOV-001;
- no code, implementation, platform, Provider, or AI output may silently redefine the baseline;
- Self-Awareness may propose but not approve or activate Foundation changes; and
- capital protection remains supreme.
