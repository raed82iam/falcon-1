## 6. Future-Stage Entry Rule

Every Stage 7 and later SHALL begin with `EXISTING_CAPABILITY_RECONCILIATION`.

If a Stage depends materially on a registered future Specification subject whose body is absent or not effective, it SHALL also complete a `SPECIFICATION_DEFINITION_REVIEW_ACTIVATION_GATE` before implementation of that subject's behavior.

## 7. Authority Rule

Roadmap placement does not grant implementation authority.

Every Stage/WP remains subject to prospective planning/design, Red-Team, Owner acceptance/authorization, implementation, verification, post-change Red-Team, and explicit Owner closure.

## 8. Historical Preservation

ROADMAP-001 v2.9 and all prior roadmap versions remain preserved in repository history as decision-time records. Their historical statements about what was blocked or incomplete at their effective instant SHALL NOT be rewritten as though they described the current repository state.
