## 4. Scope of Approval

Approval applies only to documents with content present in this baseline and whose status is **Approved**.

Registry entries that remain **Planned**, Candidate Migration, or Candidate Rewrite are not approved Specifications. They reserve governed scope only.

ADR templates remain templates. Legacy ADRs remain subject to migration review and do not acquire acceptance through this ratification.

## 5. Open Matters

An approved Specification may retain explicitly identified unresolved design or ADR matters. Approval makes its stated requirements binding; it does not approve an unstated solution.

Implementation remains unauthorized where a required contract, ADR, acceptance plan, security baseline, or dependency Specification is absent.
