# GOV-072 — Stage 1 WP-02 Evidence Recovery or Controlled Replay Authorization

## Authorization

Owner authorization is granted for bounded raw-evidence recovery and, if complete original evidence cannot be recovered, controlled rollback and evidence-complete replay of Stage 1 WP-02 only.

## Governing basis

- `GOV-071_STAGE_1_WP02_EXECUTION_AUTHORIZATION`
- `WP_02_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRES_REMEDIATION`
- `WP_02_EVIDENCE_COMPLETION_REQUIRED`
- WP-01 controlled replay acceptance and continuation records

## Scope

This authorization permits:

- bounded search for original WP-02 raw evidence;
- validation of any recovered original evidence;
- controlled rollback of only the WP-02 implementation changes if original evidence cannot be recovered;
- exact replay of the canonical WP-02 implementation only;
- creation of a new canonical replay evidence chain;
- a new independent review after recovery or replay.

## Non-authorities

- WP-03;
- new Stage 1 start;
- FIAI authority expansion;
- implementation outside WP-02;
- deletion or replacement of historical WP-02 governance, evidence, or reviews;
- fabrication or backdating of evidence;
- Git reset, clean, restore, checkout, commit, add, fetch, pull, or push;
- external package access;
- runtime, business, trading, financial, cloud, or production behavior.
