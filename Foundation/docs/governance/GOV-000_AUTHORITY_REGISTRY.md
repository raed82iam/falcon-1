## 3. Current Governance Condition

At this stage of Falcon, the Project Owner and Falcon Constitutional Authority are held by the same person.

This concentration of authority is explicitly recorded. It does not remove constitutional duties, evidence requirements, independent technical controls, or the requirement for independent approval where a Specification classifies a future action as requiring it.

No Foundation implementation Stage is currently active after Stage 9 closure. Stage 10 and all later implementation remain separately gated and `NOT AUTHORIZED` unless a later competent explicit authority says otherwise.

## 4. Authority Boundaries

The current authority holder may:

- ratify the Vision and Constitution;
- approve the Foundation Baseline;
- appoint future governance authorities;
- approve subordinate governance documents;
- authorize bounded work through explicit prospective instruments; and
- record transitions in authority and closure.

The current authority holder may not approve an action that contradicts the Vision or Constitution without first completing the lawful amendment process applicable to the higher authority.

Technical success, implementation presence, FCR existence, planning completion, an open issue, a timer, silence, a UI action, or a moving branch never creates authority by itself.

## 5. Change and Historical-Preservation Rule

Changes to role membership, delegation, mandate, or current effective work-package authority SHALL be explicit, attributable, dated, approved by the competent authority, and preserved without rewriting historical records.

This registry may reconcile current state after later valid Owner decisions while preserving the issuance-time wording of historical authority and closure instruments.

Historical details formerly summarized inline by GOV-000 v3.6 remain preserved through repository history and their canonical records. Their consolidation here is not deletion, supersession of the underlying authority record, or alteration of historical truth.
