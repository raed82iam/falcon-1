## 3. Effect

This Approval:

- activates the `BOOTSTRAP_EXTERNAL` time classification and its non-operational boundary;
- activates separation of external wall and monotonic observations;
- prohibits Falcon `VERIFIED` claims before the operational Activation boundary;
- establishes the exact CON-015 operational boundary;
- activates import and reevaluation rules without retroactive quality upgrade; and
- authorizes dependent-document and traceability updates.

## 4. Profile, Source, and Provider State

Approval of the Catalog establishes governed time meaning.

It does not activate:

- a Foundation Time Profile;
- a Clock Source or Source Set;
- a Time Provider candidate;
- an operational Time Provider; or
- Falcon `VERIFIED` Clock Quality.

Each applicable subject requires its own verification and Activation Decision.

## 5. Boundaries

This Approval does not:

- establish a Falcon Time Observation;
- convert or upgrade external or candidate time;
- establish validity, freshness, causality, ordering, identity, or authority;
- issue an Authority Instrument;
- authorize candidate construction or verification execution;
- authorize implementation;
- authorize production;
- authorize financial connectivity; or
- authorize financial activity.
