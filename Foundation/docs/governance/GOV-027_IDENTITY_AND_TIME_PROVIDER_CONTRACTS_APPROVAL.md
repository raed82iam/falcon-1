## 3. Effect

This Approval:

- admits CON-014 and CON-015 into the Foundation Contract Registry;
- establishes the exclusive governed boundaries for operational identifier issuance and time observation;
- authorizes traceability and dependent-document preparation against them; and
- supersedes CON-000 v1.2 with CON-000 v1.3.

## 4. Boundaries

This Approval does not:

- activate an Identifier Provider, Time Provider, Clock Source, or Provider Profile;
- issue an operational identifier;
- establish Falcon `VERIFIED` time;
- convert `BOOTSTRAP_EXTERNAL` identity or time into Falcon operational truth;
- grant implementation authority;
- authorize production;
- authorize financial connectivity; or
- authorize financial activity.
