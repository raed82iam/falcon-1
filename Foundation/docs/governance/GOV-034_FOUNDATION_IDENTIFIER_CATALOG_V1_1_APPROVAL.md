## 3. Effect

This Approval:

- activates the `BOOTSTRAP_EXTERNAL_ID` classification and its strict non-operational boundary;
- activates candidate-identifier isolation rules;
- establishes the exact operational Provider Activation boundary;
- requires CON-014 for new operational identifiers after that boundary;
- activates historical cross-linking without reclassification; and
- authorizes dependent-document and traceability updates.

## 4. Profile and Provider State

Approval of the Catalog establishes governed identifier meaning.

It does not activate:

- `FALCON-ID-UUID7-INTERNAL-1`;
- an Identifier Provider candidate;
- an operational Identifier Provider; or
- an environment permitted to issue operational identifiers.

Each requires its own verification and Activation Decision.

## 5. Boundaries

This Approval does not:

- issue a Falcon operational identifier;
- convert a bootstrap or candidate identifier;
- create identity, trust, admission, or authority;
- issue an Authority Instrument;
- authorize candidate construction or verification execution;
- authorize implementation;
- authorize production;
- authorize financial connectivity; or
- authorize financial activity.
