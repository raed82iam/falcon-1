## 3. Effect

This Approval:

- preserves Vision as Falcon’s highest authority;
- preserves Constitution as subordinate only to Vision;
- preserves Contracts in the document-authority order established by v1.1;
- activates the approved document-class responsibility rule;
- defines Governance, Specifications, Standards, ADRs, Contracts, Catalogs, Designs, Plans, Operational Policies, control artifacts, Reports, Authority Instruments, Implementation, Verification, Evidence, and recorded outcomes;
- separates classification, Approval, Activation, validity, Acceptance, reliance, implementation, operation, production, and financial authority;
- establishes canonical-source and document-control rules;
- establishes document-governance limits for Self-Awareness, maintenance, and evolution; and
- authorizes dependent metadata, index, registry, Roadmap, readiness, and closure-review updates.

## 4. Preserved Authority

This Approval does not:

- amend Vision or Constitution;
- change another document’s substantive meaning;
- grant jurisdiction;
- issue an Authority Instrument;
- activate a Profile, Provider, Gate, Pipeline, trace implementation, runner, or environment;
- close Foundation or AMD-003; or
- establish preparation, implementation, production, cloud, operational, or financial readiness.

## 5. Boundaries

This Approval does not:

- authorize preparation or candidate execution;
- authorize verification, build, promotion, deployment, migration, or recovery execution;
- authorize implementation;
- authorize production;
- authorize cloud deployment;
- authorize financial connectivity; or
- authorize financial activity.
