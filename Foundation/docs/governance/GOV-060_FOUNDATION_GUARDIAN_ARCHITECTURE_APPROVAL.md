## 2. Decision

The Project Owner approves:

- AMD-005 v0.1;
- ADR-I010 as the Accepted architectural boundary;
- AUT-002 v2.0 as the Approved successor design for Falcon Foundation Guardian;
- the Guardian Authority and Knowledge Matrix;
- the Registry and Migration Change Set;
- VPL-GDN-001 as an Approved verification plan whose execution is not authorized;
- the Architectural Impact Assessment; and
- the Constitutional Compliance Report.

## 3. Architectural Meaning

The Approved architecture establishes:

- FSA as Foundation awareness, diagnosis, verification, and bounded repair capability;
- FFG as Foundation technical protection and Platform Safe Mode authority;
- Foundation execution mechanisms as owners of the technical operations they execute; and
- Application Guardian as a future separately governed authority for business and capital protection.

FFG SHALL NOT interpret Application business meaning, financial state, customers, portfolios, orders, positions, strategies, or business payload.

FSA SHALL NOT release FFG restrictions or alter FFG jurisdiction. FFG SHALL NOT modify FSA or own Foundation Self-Repair or Self-Evolution.

## 4. Activation State

This approval accepts the architecture and successor design. It does not make AUT-002 v2.0 the active Specification.

AUT-002 v1.0 remains Approved and effective until:

1. affected Contracts, ADR impact records, catalogs, registries, baselines, indexes, and verification documents are prepared;
2. their consistency and historical preservation are verified;
3. the Project Owner approves a separate documentary activation record; and
4. AUT-002 v1.0 is preserved as immutable Superseded history.

No existing Approved document is silently overwritten or reinterpreted by GOV-060.

## 5. Permitted Follow-on Documentation

GOV-060 permits preparation for Owner review only of:

- technical criticality governance;
- Platform Safe Mode survival-set governance;
- mandatory trigger matrix;
- isolation boundary;
- Guardian high availability;
- independent stop channel;
- restriction persistence;
- consequence and release-authority matrix;
- maximum autonomous containment duration;
- irreversible-action quorum;
- Application-to-Foundation technical protection request Contract;
- affected Contract and verification successors; and
- registry, index, tree, glossary, and traceability updates.

Preparation does not equal approval, activation, verification execution, or implementation.

## 6. Preserved Prohibitions

GOV-060 SHALL NOT authorize:

- Stage 1 discussion, proposal preparation, commencement, or execution;
- Falcon implementation or general runtime execution;
- Guardian implementation, activation, simulation, or verification execution;
- Application Guardian creation or activation;
- code creation or modification;
- tool installation, download, or external connection;
- local or cloud deployment;
- OCI preparation or use;
- production activity;
- broker, market-data, account, capital, or financial connection; or
- any financial activity.

## 7. Mandatory Stage 1 Stop

Falcon remains before Stage 1.

Before any Stage 1 discussion, proposal preparation, or action, the Project Owner SHALL receive a clear notice that Stage 1 is a new implementation phase with new scope and risk. Stage 1 requires a separate explicit approval.
