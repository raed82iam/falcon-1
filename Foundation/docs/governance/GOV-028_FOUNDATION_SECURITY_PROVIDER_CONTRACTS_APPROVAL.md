## 3. Effect

This Approval:

- admits CON-016 through CON-019 into the Foundation Contract Registry;
- establishes the governed boundaries for cryptographic operations, secrets, certificates and identity, and randomness;
- authorizes traceability and dependent-document preparation against them; and
- supersedes CON-000 v1.3 with CON-000 v1.4.

## 4. Boundaries

This Approval does not:

- activate a Provider, Crypto Profile, custody profile, trust anchor, source, certificate, identity, secret, or key;
- create, import, or promote operational security material;
- authorize implementation;
- authorize production;
- authorize financial connectivity; or
- authorize financial activity.
