# GOV-091 Stage 3 Baseline Reconciliation and Controlled Revalidation Authority

**Identifier:** GOV-091  
**Title:** Stage 3 Baseline Reconciliation and Controlled Revalidation Authority  
**Subject:** Stage 3 baseline reconciliation, canonical metadata alignment, and controlled revalidation only  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Stage 1 status:** ACCEPTED / CLOSED  
**Stage 2 status:** ACCEPTED / CLOSED  
**Stage 3 status:** PLANNING / RECONCILIATION ONLY

## 1. Authority declaration

The Project Owner grants bounded documentary authority to reconcile the Stage 3 plan and lawful candidate evidence against the canonical lifecycle metadata of the published documents for:

- CON-023;
- APP-001;
- SYS-003;
- SYS-004;
- SYS-006;
- AWR-001;
- AWR-006;
- AWR-007; and
- AWR-008.

This authority exists only to resolve documentary inconsistency, preserve candidate implementation, and perform controlled revalidation where lawful authority already exists.

## 2. Authority scope

The authority SHALL be limited to:

- reconciling canonical metadata with already-approved effective state where the canonical record is stale;
- identifying any missing activation authority where the canonical record is not yet effective;
- preserving the current Stage 3 WP-01 and WP-02 candidate implementation as non-authoritative work pending lawful revalidation;
- correcting the Stage 3 prerequisite and contract mappings;
- updating Stage 3 readiness and reconciliation records; and
- performing independent review of the reconciliation package.

## 3. Explicit non-authorities

This authority SHALL NOT:

- retroactively authorize prior execution;
- claim execution authority for GOV-090;
- execute WP-03 or any later work package;
- modify accepted Stage 1 or Stage 2 baselines;
- introduce Application business functionality;
- start Stage 3 execution;
- start Stage 4 or later stages; or
- grant any authority beyond baseline reconciliation and controlled revalidation.

## 4. Controlled revalidation rules

- WP-01 and WP-02 evidence MAY be revalidated only after canonical reconciliation succeeds.
- Any execution and evidence records produced under prior GOV-090 planning authority remain historical and non-authoritative for execution claims.
- No file may be rewritten to imply retroactive authority.
- If a canonical document remains not effective or not authorized, the reconciliation SHALL fail closed and identify the exact missing authority.

## 5. Governing basis

- Falcon Constitution
- Falcon Document Authority
- `docs/governance/GOV-090_STAGE_3_PLANNING_AND_READINESS_AUTHORITY.md`
- `docs/stage-3-proposal/03_STAGE_3_IMPLEMENTATION_WORK_PACKAGE_PLAN.md`
- `docs/reviews/STAGE_3_WP-01_EXECUTION_REPORT_001.md`
- `docs/reviews/STAGE_3_WP-02_EXECUTION_REPORT_001.md`

## 6. Evidence obligations

Evidence produced under this authority SHALL:

- preserve the existing candidate implementation unchanged except for lawful controlled revalidation records;
- record the canonical status of each relevant document;
- distinguish historical execution claims from current authority; and
- remain documentary only.

## 7. Decision result

Stage 3 baseline reconciliation and controlled revalidation is authorized. Stage 3 execution is not authorized.
