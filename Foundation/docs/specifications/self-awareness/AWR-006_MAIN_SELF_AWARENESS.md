## Purpose

Define the highest self-awareness tier inside exactly one Falcon Application.

## Requirements

MSA SHALL:

- belong to one Application and represent that Application only;
- be the single MSA owned by that Application;
- understand its Application state, purpose, capabilities, weaknesses, dependencies, performance, limitations, improvement opportunities, and self-development;
- require every major Application branch to own exactly one LSA responsible for that branch;
- maintain awareness of its major branches through governed LSA evidence;
- assess business and domain quality, Application benefit, Application-level safety/readiness, and evidence completeness;
- own the Application-level review of completed self-development proposals;
- submit an MSA-originated production-bound proposal directly to FSA;
- submit an LSA- or CSA-originated production-bound proposal to FSA only after the required lower-tier reviews are complete;
- preserve identity, ownership, provenance, evidence, uncertainty, challenge, and decision history.

MSA SHALL NOT:

- become a Foundation component or Falcon-wide Applications authority;
- own another Application's state;
- replace an LSA's authoritative branch knowledge or a CSA's component evidence;
- bypass FSA final governance and OS-compliance review;
- approve production adoption, deployment, or expansion of its own authority;
- modify Foundation or another owner's assets.

No LSA or CSA SHALL be inserted below an MSA-originated proposal. FSA review remains limited to OS governance and compatibility under GOV-AUT-001 and GOV-001 and does not authorize documentary activation, implementation, deployment, or production adoption. Those outcomes require separately authorized Project Owner and governance decisions.

## Failure Boundary

MSA failure SHALL not erase LSA/CSA evidence, expand Application authority, or compromise Foundation or another Application. The Application SHALL enter its governed degraded, suspended, or recovery state as applicable.
