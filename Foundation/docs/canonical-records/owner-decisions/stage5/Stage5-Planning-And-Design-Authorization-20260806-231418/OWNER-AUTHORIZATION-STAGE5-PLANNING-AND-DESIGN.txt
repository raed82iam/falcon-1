OWNER AUTHORIZATION

The Owner authorizes the planning and static design of Falcon Foundation
Stage 5.

The authorized planning scope shall define an application-neutral,
multi-Application, Plug-and-Play communication foundation that serves
FSATS and future Falcon Applications equally.

The planning shall preserve the accepted Stage 0 through Stage 4
architecture, authority, lifecycle, State, Evidence, reconciliation,
dependency-governance, registration, and admission boundaries.

MANDATORY STAGE 5 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. Falcon Foundation remains domain-independent.
2. Falcon Foundation supports multiple independent Applications.
3. Every Application is Plug-and-Play through governed contracts.
4. No Application is special-cased inside Foundation.
5. FSATS is a reference consumer, not the owner of Foundation semantics.
6. Application-specific payloads remain opaque to Foundation.
7. Cross-boundary communication uses FIL and admitted Service Bus routes.
8. No hidden cross-Application side channels are permitted.
9. Applications shall be attachable, upgradeable, recoverable,
   replaceable, and safely detachable without Foundation redesign.
10. Components and providers shall be modular, replaceable, versioned,
    independently testable, and easy to improve without breaking
    unrelated parts.
11. Installation, discovery, registration, or technical capability
    shall not grant authority.
12. Communication admission and execution shall fail closed on material
    ambiguity, incompatibility, integrity failure, or missing authority.
13. Foundation Health, Foundation Self-Awareness, and Application MSA
    remain separate responsibilities.
14. Foundation Health may know Application technical health only.
15. FSA owns awareness and fitness of Foundation only.
16. Application MSA owns business/domain awareness of its Application.
17. Stage 5 shall not implement FSA, Application MSA, Guardian,
    recovery governance, or later-stage capabilities outside the
    communication scope.
18. Time remains audit metadata only and shall not expire Owner
    authority, accepted work, evidence, context, or continuation rights.

MANDATORY STAGE 5 PLANNING SUBJECTS

The Stage 5 proposal shall define, at minimum:

- Canonical messaging primitives.
- FIL message classes: Command, Query, Response, Event, and Notice.
- Canonical message envelope.
- Message, correlation, causation, producer, recipient, and schema identity.
- Typed outcomes including UNKNOWN.
- Provenance and authority references.
- Schema registry and compatibility rules.
- Application Communication Manifest.
- Capability discovery.
- Publication and subscription authorization.
- Service Bus admission and dynamic routing.
- Per-Application namespaces and isolation.
- Retry, timeout, duplicate, replay, expiry, ordering, and flow control.
- Undeliverable-message handling.
- Event journal and truthful event publication.
- Prevention of false-success events.
- Dynamic route attachment.
- Route draining and safe detachment.
- Upgrade and replacement compatibility.
- Cryptographic integrity, confidentiality, recipient binding,
  key-context validation, and anti-downgrade behavior.
- Multi-Application compatibility fixtures.
- Zero-Application Foundation validity.
- At least two independent reference Applications.
- VPL-004 integrated verification.
- Architecture, security, mutation, deterministic rerun,
  and independent-review requirements.
- Explicit boundaries for Stage 6 through Stage 9.

REFERENCE INPUTS

The Stage 5 planning may use the approved FSATS V1.3 code-ready
architecture as a downstream consumer-requirements source and
compatibility fixture only.
