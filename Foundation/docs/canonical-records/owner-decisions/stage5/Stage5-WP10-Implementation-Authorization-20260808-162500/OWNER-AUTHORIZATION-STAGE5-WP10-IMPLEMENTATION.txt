AUTHORIZED PURPOSE

Perform the bounded final Stage 5 integration, consistency, end-to-end verification and closure-readiness work necessary to prove that accepted Stage 5 WP-01 through WP-09 compose correctly as one Application-neutral Foundation integration layer, without inventing new business semantics or silently adding capabilities outside the accepted Stage 5 design.

WP-10 is an integration/closure work package. It may add only the minimum generic integration glue, evidence, verification, compatibility checks or fail-closed controls strictly necessary to make the already accepted WP-01 through WP-09 boundaries compose correctly. Any newly discovered requirement that is materially outside those accepted boundaries SHALL NOT be smuggled into WP-10; it SHALL be classified as a separate FCR, deferred capability, later-stage concern, or explicit new authority request as appropriate.

FOUNDATION INDEPENDENCE

Foundation remains Application-neutral, valid with zero Applications, and independent of Trading or any other concrete Application.

Foundation may verify that Application requests and declared integration behavior comply with Vision, Constitution, governance, authority, isolation, resources, FIL/contracts, Service Bus, security, lifecycle and other generic platform rules. Foundation SHALL NOT own or interpret Application business semantics, business outcomes, strategy logic, Trading Risk logic, broker/provider choice, market meaning, portfolio logic or other Application-owned decisions.

Any FCR or integration request that attempts to transfer Application business semantics or business-decision authority into Foundation SHALL be rejected in that form with the violated governance/architecture boundary stated. A legitimate underlying platform need may be reformulated as an Application-neutral capability.
