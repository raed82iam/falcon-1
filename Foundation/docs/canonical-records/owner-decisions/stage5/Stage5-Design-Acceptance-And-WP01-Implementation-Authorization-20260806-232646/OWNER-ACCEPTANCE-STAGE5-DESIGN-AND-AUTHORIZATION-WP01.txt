OWNER DESIGN ACCEPTANCE

The Owner confirms review and acceptance of the Stage 5 planning and
design package identified above.

The accepted Stage 5 design establishes an application-neutral,
multi-Application, Plug-and-Play communication foundation.

The accepted architecture includes:

- Canonical messaging primitives.
- FIL Command, Query, Response, Event, and Notice classes.
- Canonical message envelope.
- Schema registry and compatibility governance.
- Application Communication Manifest.
- Publication and subscription authorization.
- Service Bus admission and dynamic routing.
- Per-Application isolation and namespaces.
- Retry, duplicate, replay, expiry, ordering, flow control,
  and undeliverable-message handling.
- Truthful event publication and prevention of false-success events.
- Cryptographic integrity, confidentiality, recipient binding,
  and anti-downgrade behavior.
- Dynamic Application attachment, upgrade, replacement,
  draining, and safe detachment.
- Zero-Application Foundation validity.
- Multi-Application compatibility verification.
- Integrated VPL-004 closure verification.
- Separation of Foundation Health, FSA, and Application MSA.
- Modular, replaceable, versioned, and independently testable parts.

The Stage 5 design does not make FSATS the owner or privileged consumer
of Foundation communication semantics.

FSATS remains a reference consumer and compatibility fixture only.

WP-01 PURPOSE

WP-01 shall establish the application-neutral canonical primitives
required by all later Stage 5 work packages.

WP-01 AUTHORIZED SCOPE

WP-01 may implement only the approved foundational contracts,
value objects, validators, and verification coverage required for:

1. Canonical message identity.
2. Producer identity reference.
3. Intended recipient or recipient-scope reference.
4. Correlation identity.
5. Causation identity.
6. Schema identity and schema-version reference.
7. Authority reference.
8. Provenance reference.
9. Message classification.
10. Message kind classification:
    Command, Query, Response, Event, Notice.
11. Typed outcome model including UNKNOWN.
12. Canonical time metadata representation.
13. Expiry metadata representation without changing the rule that
    time does not expire Owner authority, accepted evidence, or context.
14. Idempotency identity.
15. Delivery-attempt and retry-lineage identity where required
    by the accepted design.
16. Canonical immutable message-envelope contract.
17. Deterministic equality, serialization boundaries,
    validation, and identity behavior.
18. Fail-closed rejection of malformed or ambiguous primitives.
19. Architecture tests.
20. Security tests.
21. WP-01 verifier and deterministic rerun evidence.
22. Documentation and traceability for WP-01 only.

WP-01 MANDATORY BOUNDARIES

WP-01 shall remain:

- application-neutral;
- domain-independent;
- free of trading, accounting, communication, web,
  or other Application business semantics;
- free of hardcoded Application names or routes;
- modular and replaceable behind approved contracts;
- compatible with multiple independent Plug-and-Play Applications;
- compatible with a zero-Application Foundation;
- consistent with accepted Stage 0 through Stage 4 authority,
  lifecycle, State, Evidence, registration, admission,
  dependency-governance, and reconciliation behavior.

WP-01 shall not introduce:

- a Service Bus implementation;
- dynamic routing;
- schema-registry persistence;
- publish/subscribe execution;
- event-journal execution;
- cryptographic transport;
- Application attachment or detachment execution;
- FSA;
- Application MSA;
- Guardian;
- recovery governance;
- business payload interpretation;
- a second authority controller;
- a second lifecycle controller;
- a second authoritative State owner;
- a second Evidence owner.

REQUIRED WP-01 VERIFICATION

Before WP-01 may be presented for Owner acceptance, evidence shall show:

- clean Release build;
- architecture tests pass;
- security tests pass;
- Stage 0 through Stage 4 accepted verifiers remain passing;
- canonical primitive positive scenarios pass;
- malformed primitive scenarios fail closed;
- UNKNOWN cannot be silently converted to success or failure;
- message kinds cannot be confused or substituted;
- correlation and causation identities remain distinct;
- producer and recipient identities cannot be mutated undetected;
- schema identity and version remain bound;
- idempotency identity remains deterministic;
- immutable envelope behavior is verified;
- application-neutrality is verified;
- no FSATS-specific or other domain-specific logic exists;
- deterministic rerun produces equivalent accepted results;
- independent completeness review is completed.
