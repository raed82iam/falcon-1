OWNER AUTHORIZATION
The Owner authorizes implementation of:

Stage 5 WP-04 - FIL Validation and Message Admission

WP-04 PURPOSE
WP-04 shall establish the application-neutral, deterministic, attributable, reconstructable, fail-closed FIL validation and message-admission decision surface that determines whether a canonical WP-01 message may cross the bounded admission gate under accepted WP-02 schema rules, accepted WP-03 Manifest declarations, accepted Stage 4 authority decisions, and applicable deterministic temporal constraints.

WP-04 admission is a decision only. It shall not route, dispatch, queue, deliver, publish, execute, retry, deduplicate operational effects, create transport connectivity, or interpret Application business payload meaning.

AUTHORIZED WP-04 SCOPE
WP-04 may implement only the source, immutable decision structures, validators/evaluators, deterministic canonicalization/digest behavior, documentation, architecture/security coverage, fixtures, and verification required for:

1. Consuming the accepted WP-01 CanonicalFilEnvelope without redefining WP-01 semantics.
2. Separating structural FIL validity, schema validity/compatibility, authority, admission, domain validity, and execution states.
3. Validating canonical message identity, kind, type, classification, producer identity, recipient scope, schema identity/version, authority reference, provenance, correlation/causation, time/expiry, and other accepted WP-01 material admission metadata.
4. Resolving schema identity/version through the accepted WP-02 ISchemaRegistry.
5. Applying accepted WP-02 explicit schema compatibility behavior where a governed declared version and received message version differ.
6. Rejecting unknown, retired, undeclared, or incompatible schema use as applicable.
7. Resolving and validating the applicable accepted WP-03 Application Communication Manifest.
8. Binding each message unambiguously to the applicable Application/Manifest identity and communication declaration.
9. Checking message type, kind, classification, schema, direction, role, producer, and applicable recipient/consumer declaration consistency without creating a route.
10. Rejecting missing, undeclared, ambiguous, duplicate, or conflicting communication bindings fail closed.
11. Reusing the accepted Stage 4 DefaultDenyAuthorityEngine / AuthorityResult surface rather than creating a second authority engine.
12. Requiring explicit attributable accepted authority for admission where authority is required by the canonical message/declaration.
13. Rejecting missing, denied, stale, expired, mismatched, malformed, or unverifiable required authority input fail closed.
14. Enforcing message expiry at an explicitly supplied deterministic observation time without using hidden wall-clock authority.
15. Preserving the distinction between message expiry and the governance TIME PRINCIPLE; Owner authority and accepted work do not expire merely because audit time advances.
16. Producing an immutable admission result containing decision identity, message identity, relevant Manifest/schema/authority bindings, decision, exact reason, observation time, bounded expiry, and evidence identity/reference.
17. Deterministic canonicalization and SHA-256 binding of material admission inputs and outputs.
18. Mutation sensitivity for every material input that can change an admission decision.
19. Explicit rejection reason codes with no ambiguous generic success state.
20. Proving that ADMITTED means only WP-04 admission passed and does not mean route creation, dispatch, delivery, consumer acceptance, command execution, event truth, business success, deployment approval, or production approval.
21. Preserving Application payload opacity; Foundation shall not parse or interpret Application business payload meaning.
22. Zero-Application Foundation compatibility.
23. At least two independent Application-neutral admission fixtures with no FSATS special treatment.
24. Compatibility with accepted Stage 0 through Stage 4 behavior and accepted/closed Stage 5 WP-01 through WP-03.
25. Dedicated WP-04 verification, architecture/security coverage, deterministic rerun, documentation, traceability, independent architecture review, independent red-team review, independent completeness review, and final evidence reconciliation.

MANDATORY WP-04 PRINCIPLES

- Default deny.
- No inferred authority.
- Authentication or identity presence is not action authority.
- Structural validity is not schema validity.
- Schema validity/compatibility is not authority.
- Manifest validity is not message admission.
- Message admission is not route creation.
- Message admission is not delivery.
- Message admission is not execution.
- Transport success, when later implemented, is not business truth.
- Application business payload meaning remains opaque to Foundation.
- All material rejection states are explicit and attributable.
- Deterministic supplied evidence and observation inputs shall be used; no hidden mutable clock or ambient authority may make replay nondeterministic.
- Existing accepted owners remain singular: WP-01 owns canonical messaging primitives; WP-02 owns schema registration/compatibility; WP-03 owns Manifest declaration/validation; Stage 4 Authority owns authority decisions; WP-04 owns only the bounded message-admission decision.

FCR BOUNDARY
Open FCRs remain governed request/disposition records only and do not grant implementation authority.

FCR-0004, FCR-0005, FCR-0006, and FCR-0009 may be used only as generic downstream compatibility fixtures where their needs intersect the already-authorized WP-04 admission surface. They shall not expand WP-04 into routing, delivery, replay delivery, event publication, QoS transport, backpressure, resource governance, or Application-specific behavior.
