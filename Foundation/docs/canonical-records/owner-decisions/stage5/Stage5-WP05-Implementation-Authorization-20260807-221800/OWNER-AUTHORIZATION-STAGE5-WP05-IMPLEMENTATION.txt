OWNER AUTHORIZATION
The Owner authorizes implementation of:

Stage 5 WP-05 - Service Bus Dynamic Routing and Isolation

WP-05 PURPOSE
WP-05 shall establish an Application-neutral, deterministic, attributable, auditable, fail-closed Service Bus route declaration, route-eligibility, route-selection, and route/endpoint-isolation decision surface for messages that have already passed the accepted WP-04 message-admission boundary.

WP-05 shall not deliver messages. It shall determine only whether a governed route is valid and eligible and, where authorized by explicit deterministic policy, which one governed route is selected.

AUTHORIZED WP-05 SCOPE
WP-05 may implement only the source, immutable models, registries/resolvers where needed, validators/evaluators, deterministic canonicalization/digest behavior, documentation, architecture/security coverage, fixtures, and verification required for:

1. Reusing the accepted WP-04 admission result without redefining admission semantics.
2. Immutable route identity and explicit route declarations.
3. Versioned route declaration/resolution if required for deterministic identity.
4. Explicit producer/source endpoint and consumer/destination endpoint binding.
5. Explicit route binding to technical FIL/Manifest metadata including message type, destination/recipient scope, declared purpose, and accepted authority/admission evidence where applicable.
6. Default-deny route eligibility.
7. Explicit route state sufficient to distinguish eligible from isolated/unavailable/ineligible routes without executing lifecycle transitions.
8. Explicit endpoint eligibility/isolation sufficient to prevent selection of an affected source/destination while preserving unrelated routes.
9. Deterministic route selection from declared eligible candidates only.
10. Fail-closed no-route behavior.
11. Fail-closed ambiguous-route behavior where deterministic policy does not establish exactly one winner.
12. Rejection of unknown, undeclared, duplicate, conflicting, stale, isolated, mismatched, or unverifiable route bindings.
13. Preservation of original producer identity, message identity, correlation, causation, classification, provenance, schema, authority/admission binding, and payload opacity.
14. No Application-name parsing or business-payload interpretation for routing.
15. Immutable route decision containing route identity, relevant source/destination/admission/authority/policy/state/evidence bindings, exact decision/reason, deterministic observation inputs where applicable, and decision identity.
16. Deterministic canonicalization and SHA-256 binding of material routing inputs and outputs.
17. Mutation sensitivity for every material input that can change route eligibility or route selection.
18. Route-failure/isolation containment such that isolating one route/endpoint does not silently invalidate unrelated independent routes.
19. Zero-Application Foundation compatibility.
20. At least two independent Application-neutral routing fixtures with no FSATS special treatment.
21. Generic compatibility with FCR-0004/0005/0006/0009 only where their needs intersect this already-authorized route-selection/isolation surface.
22. Dedicated WP-05 verification, Architecture/Security coverage, deterministic rerun, documentation, traceability, independent architecture review, independent red-team review, independent completeness review, and final evidence/FCR reconciliation.

MANDATORY WP-05 PRINCIPLES
- Default deny.
- Route existence is not authority.
- Technical reachability is not admission or activation.
- WP-04 admission is a prerequisite input, not a routing grant.
- Routing shall use declared governed technical metadata only.
- Routing shall never inspect Application business payload meaning.
- No Application receives privileged routing behavior.
- Hidden direct Application-to-Application coupling is prohibited.
- Isolation shall be contained to the governed affected route/endpoint to the required degree.
- No hidden wall-clock input shall make deterministic route replay nondeterministic.
- Existing accepted owners remain singular and unchanged.

FCR BOUNDARY
Open FCRs remain request/disposition records only and do not grant implementation authority.

FCR-0004 may inform generic governed protection-command route requirements only up to route eligibility/selection/isolation. It does not authorize command delivery/execution, protective QoS, acknowledgement, or congestion behavior.

FCR-0005 may inform generic producer/consumer/schema/route eligibility only. Operational delivery semantics remain outside WP-05.

FCR-0006 may inform generic route attribution/isolation only. Event truth, event publication, replay delivery, ordering/correction execution, and evidence-retention behavior remain outside WP-05.

FCR-0009 may inform route eligibility from already-governed expiry/deadline/technical metadata only. Queueing, backpressure, scheduling, overload handling, delivery QoS, and tail-latency behavior remain outside WP-05.
