OWNER AUTHORIZATION CANDIDATE

Upon explicit Owner acceptance of this candidate as an effective prospective authorization, the Owner would authorize implementation of:

Stage 5 WP-03 - Application Communication Manifest

This candidate is not itself implementation authority.

WP-03 PURPOSE

WP-03 shall establish the application-neutral, immutable, attributable, deterministic, and fail-closed Application Communication Manifest model required to declare an Application's governed communication intent and dependencies before later Stage 5 admission, routing, delivery, event, cryptographic, and Plug-and-Play execution work.

WP-03 shall implement declaration and validation only. It shall not execute communication, grant authority, admit messages, create routes, deliver messages, publish events, activate Applications, or interpret Application business payload meaning.

WP-03 AUTHORIZED SCOPE AFTER OWNER ACCEPTANCE

WP-03 may implement only the approved contracts, immutable manifest structures, validators, canonicalization/digest behavior, deterministic snapshot/reconstruction behavior where required, documentation, architecture/security coverage, fixtures, and verification necessary for:

1. A canonical Application Communication Manifest identity and explicit manifest version.
2. Binding each manifest to one immutable Application identity and Application version.
3. Binding each manifest to one attributable Application owner or governing owner reference without transferring Application ownership to Foundation.
4. Declaring communication intent independently from communication execution.
5. Declaring required Foundation communication contracts and services by canonical governed reference.
6. Declaring provided communication capabilities and intended consumers by stable, Application-neutral references.
7. Declaring allowed communication directions and roles without creating transport routes or runtime connectivity.
8. Declaring WP-01 canonical message identities, message kinds, classifications, or other canonical communication primitives where required by the accepted Stage 5 design.
9. Declaring referenced schemas by canonical WP-01 SchemaIdentity and explicit WP-02 schema version.
10. Validating referenced schema identities and versions against the accepted WP-02 Schema Registry without implementing message admission.
11. Declaring required permissions and authority-request references without granting, inferring, or activating authority.
12. Declaring communication-related security-profile requirements without implementing WP-08 cryptographic transport or message protection.
13. Declaring communication dependencies and compatible versions without replacing existing Foundation Dependency Governance authority.
14. Declaring required communication configuration and evidence references where required by CON-023 without creating runtime configuration authority.
15. Declaring communication-related lifecycle applicability needed to identify manifest validity across Application version change, update, replacement, suspension, or removal without implementing those lifecycle transitions.
16. Explicitly separating manifest validity from registration, admission, activation, routing, delivery, business approval, production approval, or runtime reachability.
17. Fail-closed rejection of malformed, incomplete, ambiguous, internally conflicting, duplicate, or non-canonical manifest declarations.
18. Fail-closed rejection of unknown or undeclared schema references when the accepted WP-02 registry cannot resolve the referenced schema identity/version.
19. Prevention of duplicate manifest identity registration where such registration is required by the bounded WP-03 implementation.
20. Prevention of conflicting Application identity, Application version, manifest version, owner reference, contract reference, schema reference, permission request, or communication-declaration binding.
21. Deterministic canonicalization and SHA-256 binding of accepted manifest content.
22. Deterministic manifest equality and reconstruction behavior where exposed by the bounded implementation.
23. Preservation of Application business payload opacity. Foundation shall not interpret trading, market, broker, accounting, web, communication-application, or other domain semantics.
24. Zero-Application Foundation compatibility.
25. At least two independent Application-neutral manifest fixtures proving no Application receives privileged Foundation treatment.
26. Positive manifest-validation fixtures.
27. Negative, ambiguity, conflict, mutation, and malformed-input fixtures.
28. Architecture tests covering Foundation/Application ownership, Application neutrality, no hidden cross-Application coupling, and no prohibited dependency direction.
29. Security tests covering fail-closed declaration validation and prevention of authority inference from manifest presence or validity.
30. Deterministic WP-03 verifier rerun.
31. WP-03 documentation and requirement-to-verifier traceability.

MANDATORY WP-03 MANIFEST PRINCIPLES

Every manifest shall be explicit, attributable, immutable at its accepted public surface, versioned, deterministic, and reconstructable where reconstruction is part of the bounded implementation.

Every manifest shall bind to one Application identity and explicit Application version.

Every manifest shall preserve one attributable Application owner or governing owner reference.

Communication declaration shall not equal communication execution.

Manifest presence or validity shall not imply installation, registration, admission, authority, activation, route creation, transport reachability, business approval, production approval, or deployment approval.

Schema references shall use the accepted WP-01 and WP-02 canonical schema identities and version rules.

Manifest validation shall fail closed on malformed, ambiguous, conflicting, unresolved, or undeclared required references.

Foundation shall remain valid and governable with zero Applications.

Multiple independent Applications shall be supportable without Application-specific Foundation branches.

No Application, including FSATS, shall be privileged by the WP-03 implementation.

Foundation shall not interpret Application business payload semantics.

Direct hidden Application-to-Application coupling shall remain prohibited.
