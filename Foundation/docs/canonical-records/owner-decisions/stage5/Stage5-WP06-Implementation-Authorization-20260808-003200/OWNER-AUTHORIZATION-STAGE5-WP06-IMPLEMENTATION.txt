AUTHORIZED SCOPE

WP-06 may implement only the bounded Application-neutral Service Bus delivery-semantics and flow-control layer following an accepted WP-05 route-selection decision, including:

- explicit transport delivery guarantees/modes without overstating business completion;
- immutable delivery-attempt identity and lineage;
- truthful dispatch/acknowledgement/delivery/terminal outcome distinction;
- bounded retry decisions respecting expiry, idempotency expectations and governed destination/route health;
- explicit dead-letter/terminal containment for undeliverable messages with reason and evidence;
- explicit scoped ordering guarantees and ordering keys where ordering is claimed;
- bounded flow-control/backpressure/congestion decisions preserving cross-route and cross-producer isolation;
- governed technical priority handling that prohibits producer self-elevation;
- deterministic protective/revocation communication behavior under congestion/degradation where governed policy permits;
- deterministic immutable delivery/retry/dead-letter/flow-control evidence and decision identities;
- dedicated verifier, architecture/security integration, CI integration and documentation needed to validate this exact WP-06 scope.

PREDECESSOR RULE

WP-06 SHALL consume accepted predecessor decisions and SHALL NOT bypass, duplicate, weaken, or recreate:

- WP-03 Manifest declaration/validation;
- WP-04 FIL message-admission authority;
- WP-05 route declaration, route authority, route eligibility, deterministic route selection, or route/endpoint isolation ownership.

- WP-07 event publication/subscription truth, event ownership, replay truth, or event-journal implementation;
- WP-08 cryptographic message/channel protection, encryption, signatures, key management, or protection-profile implementation;
- WP-09 Application package installation, attachment, activation, upgrade, replacement, draining, detachment, or removal lifecycle;

- Application-specific or trading-specific Foundation branches;
- interpretation of business/financial payload meaning;
- claims of business success/completion from transport status;

- merge to another branch or tag creation;
- Stage 6 through Stage 9 implementation.
