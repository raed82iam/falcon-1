# VPL-BST-006 — Environment Activation Verification Plan

**Identifier:** VPL-BST-006  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Approval Record:** GOV-031  
**Owner:** Falcon Verification Authority  
**Governing Sources:** ENV-001; ADR-I008; SEC-002; CON-008; CON-010; CON-012; CON-020; CON-021  
**Master Plan:** VPL-BST-000  
**Implementation Authority:** Not Granted

## 1. Objective

Prove that one exact Preparation, Candidate Provider Verification, Foundation Build Verification, Reproducibility, or Evidence Review environment can be activated only for its declared scope through complete independent evidence and competent Activation Authority.

## 2. Required Setup

- exact environment candidate, image, configuration, capabilities, network, storage, and cleanup identity;
- applicable passed prerequisite plans;
- immutable candidate Manifest;
- Evidence Requirement Set and Root Evidence Set;
- Validity Assessment and Completeness Decision;
- independent review;
- Profile Activation Authority Instrument; and
- controlled invalid variants.

## 3. Procedure

1. verify the exact environment class and candidate identity.
2. reconstruct preparation and verification evidence.
3. evaluate capabilities, isolation, identity, time, custody, tools, inputs, cleanup, and export.
4. verify applicable Provider dependencies and states.
5. verify authority jurisdiction, independence, scope, and expiry.
6. issue a controlled Activation Decision for the exact subject.
7. verify active scope and all non-authorities.
8. test wrong image, profile, environment, evidence set, authority, Provider state, and expired decision.
9. test restriction, suspension, revocation, and no self-restoration.

## 4. Pass Rule

`PASS` requires exact bounded Activation, rejection of every mismatched or incomplete case, enforceable restriction and revocation, preserved non-authorities, and complete reconstruction.

## 5. Requirements

- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-001:** Activation SHALL identify one exact environment subject, class, version, and scope.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-002:** Every applicable prerequisite Evidence Requirement Set SHALL be complete.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-003:** Validity, completeness, and Activation SHALL remain separate decisions.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-004:** Candidate producer and environment preparer SHALL not be sole Activation Authority.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-005:** Wrong or incomplete identity, evidence, authority, profile, or dependency SHALL prevent Activation.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-006:** Activation SHALL preserve explicit non-authorities.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-007:** Restriction, suspension, revocation, and expiry SHALL be enforceable.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-008:** Environment return SHALL not cause automatic restoration.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-009:** Environment Activation SHALL not imply Foundation Implementation or operational authority.
- **VPL-BST-006-REQ-010:** The complete Activation case SHALL be reconstructable.
