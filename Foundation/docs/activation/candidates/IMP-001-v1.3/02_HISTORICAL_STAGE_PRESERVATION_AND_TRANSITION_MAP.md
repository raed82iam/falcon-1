## Closure-defect rule

A historical closure SHALL be challenged only if independent evidence proves that an obligation belonged to its exact authorized/accepted scope and was not satisfied.

A later requirement, planned Specification, FCR, capability family, environment, Application use case, or broader operational claim is not by itself a closure defect.

## Supersession semantics

If IMP-001 v1.3 is activated:

1. IMP-001 v1.2 remains preserved as the historical controlling predecessor for work executed under it.
2. Accepted evidence is not relabeled under v1.3.
3. New Stage numbering applies prospectively to remaining/future work only.
4. Historical verification keeps its original assurance meaning.
5. No completed authority instrument is revived or extended.
6. No completed WP becomes re-authorized by documentary supersession.
