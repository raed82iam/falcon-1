## 1. Scope

Review the complete IMP-001 v1.3 successor candidate package against the Falcon Vision, Falcon Constitution, Documentation Authority, authority/jurisdiction rules, and accepted Foundation/Application boundaries.

## 2. Vision Alignment

The package preserves Falcon as a Self-Aware Autonomous Financial Operating System serving the ordered objective Protect Capital → Manage Capital → Grow Capital.

The Foundation roadmap remains non-financial in implementation scope and does not redefine Falcon as a Windows product, cloud product, trading system, Application, broker, or AI product.

**Result:** PASS.

## 3. Constitution and Authority Alignment

The package:

- does not create authority from planning;
- does not create implementation authority from document activation;
- preserves explicit Owner authorization gates;
- preserves challengeability, evidence and independent review;
- preserves the rule that intelligence/self-awareness does not create authority;
- preserves Guardian as protective rather than general authority;
- preserves recovery/release separation;
- preserves explicit delegation and rejects silence as approval;
- preserves historical approved records and requires governed supersession.

**Result:** PASS.

## 4. Separation of Responsibilities

The package preserves:

- Foundation as OS/platform authority, not Application business owner;
- FSA as Foundation/OS awareness only;
- MSA/LSA/CSA inside Applications;
- Resource Governance as Foundation resource-truth owner;
- Guardian distinct from FSA, Recovery and Resource Governance;
- Application business logic, financial decision meaning and Trading semantics outside Foundation.

**Result:** PASS.

## 5. Environment Neutrality

The package treats environment neutrality as a Foundation-wide invariant and Stage 16 only as realization/qualification evidence.

No OS/cloud/provider becomes constitutional, governance, truth or business authority.

Evidence remains scoped to the environment actually verified.

**Result:** PASS.

## 6. Zero-Application Foundation

The package requires Foundation to remain valid with zero Applications and treats Applications as Plug-and-Play consumers.

No Application becomes a prerequisite for Foundation identity, lifecycle, authority, Health, FSA, Guardian, Recovery, resource truth or communication infrastructure.

**Result:** PASS.

## 7. Historical Closure Integrity

The package does not reopen Stage 0A through Stage 5 or Stage 6 WP-01 through WP-04.

Later obligations, future Specifications and post-FRS capabilities are not retroactively classified as closure defects.

**Result:** PASS.

## 8. FRS-001 Integrity

FRS-001 remains the first non-financial Foundation release and closes at corrected Stage 10.

Stages 11 through Stage 17 remain post-FRS Foundation platform capabilities and do not silently broaden FRS-001.

VPL-000 and VPL-001 through VPL-008 retain their FRS meaning.

**Result:** PASS.

## 9. Change Governance

The package uses prospective successor candidates for IMP, ROADMAP and TRC; preserves prior versions; prepares a governed AWR-001 documentary-only remediation; and requires coordinated activation rather than silent in-place reinterpretation.

**Result:** PASS.

## 10. Adverse Interpretation Tests

Rejected interpretations include:

- “Stage appears in plan, therefore implementation is authorized.”
- “Windows was first, therefore Falcon is a Windows architecture.”
- “No Applications means Foundation is incomplete.”
- “External research loss means FSA ceases to exist.”
- “FRS completion means financial or enterprise readiness.”
- “A planned Specification row supplies detailed requirements.”
- “An open FCR grants implementation authority.”
- “A later requirement makes an accepted closure defective by default.”

All are explicitly rejected by the candidate package.
