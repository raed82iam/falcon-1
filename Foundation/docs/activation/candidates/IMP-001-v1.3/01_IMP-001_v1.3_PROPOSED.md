## 1. Purpose

IMP-001 v1.3 proposes one coherent Foundation implementation sequence from the preserved accepted baseline through the complete currently known non-financial Foundation platform program.

It preserves accepted Stage history, preserves current Stage 6 Resource Governance, restores the still-required historical Stage 6 through Stage 9 purposes as Stages 7 through 10, and orders the currently known post-FRS Foundation platform capability families through Stage 17.

No Stage in this document is authorized merely because it is listed.

Purpose: complete Foundation technical protective restriction, Safe State, persistence of restrictions and independent release boundaries.

### Stage 9 — Controlled Recovery and Independent Release

Purpose: complete governed restoration, reconciliation, independent recovery validation, controlled reintroduction and separate release authority.

### Stage 10 — Full FRS-001 Reconstruction and Foundation Release Review

Purpose: close the corrected FRS-001 non-financial sequence through complete reconstruction, traceability, constitutional/security/authority review, recovery evidence, known-limitations inventory and Release Authority decision.

Stage 10 closes FRS-001 only. It does not claim post-FRS platform operational readiness or financial readiness.

### Stage 11 — Transport QoS, Deadline Governance and Observability

Purpose: provide generic observable and bounded transport-performance governance consuming Stage 5 delivery/event truth and Stage 6 resource/priority/pressure truth.

### Stage 12 — Governed External Access, Egress and Credential-Reference Security

Purpose: provide one generic fail-closed external-access substrate with independently authorized service roles for research, non-Live restrictions, operational provider/data-service access and broker-execution access.

FSA core operation does not depend on egress. Research capability may consume this Stage when authorized.

### Stage 13 — FSA / Owner Governance and Bounded Self-Maintenance & Evolution Control Plane

Purpose: govern FSA/Owner interaction, proposal intake, delegation validation, maintenance/evolution classification, candidate provenance, bounded promotion, rollback and independent adoption authority.

Owner silence and timer expiry do not create authority.

### Stage 14 — Canonical Foundation Artifact Publication and Application Consumption

Purpose: publish and consume exact accepted Foundation artifacts across separated workstreams without source copying, local forks, moving-branch dependency or unverifiable package identity.

Publication is not activation. Consumption is not authority.

### Stage 15 — Application Runtime Hosting, Admission, Activation and Capability Isolation

Purpose: provide the generic runtime host for zero or more Plug-and-Play Applications using accepted communication/lifecycle truth, Stage 6 resource grants and Stage 14 artifact consumption.

Zero Applications is a valid state. Application presence is never a Foundation prerequisite.

### Stage 16 — Environment-Neutral Runtime Qualification and Deployment Realization

Purpose: qualify the same environment-neutral Foundation architecture in each declared execution environment without changing Foundation semantics.

Windows, Linux, OCI and future environments are separately evidenced realizations, not separate Falcon architectures.

Evidence from one environment does not establish another environment as valid.

### Stage 17 — Standalone Foundation Operational Readiness and Zero-Application Acceptance

Purpose: establish that Falcon Foundation can operate as a governed standalone non-financial platform with zero Applications and can add/remove conforming Applications without Foundation redesign.

Mandatory scenarios include zero-Application cold start, steady state, restart/recovery, first Application admission, rejection of a non-conforming Application, Application failure isolation, removal back to zero and operation in every environment claimed operational under Stage 16.

Stage 17 creates no financial authority.

## 6. Mandatory future-Stage gates

Every Stage 7 through Stage 17 SHALL begin with `EXISTING_CAPABILITY_RECONCILIATION`.

Every material planned Specification subject lacking an effective Specification body SHALL pass `SPECIFICATION_DEFINITION_REVIEW_ACTIVATION_GATE` before its behavior is implemented.

## 7. Traceability

The controlling traceability baseline SHALL be updated before activation of this proposed successor so that every Stage, requirement family, Contract, ADR, verification family, FCR planning target, historical closure and non-authority is represented without relabeling historical evidence.

## 8. Completion model

- Stage 10 completion = FRS-001 complete within its approved non-financial scope.
- Stage 17 completion = Foundation standalone non-financial platform operational-readiness claim within exact admitted environment(s) and authority.
- Neither completion grants trading, broker, market-data, capital, investment or other financial authority.

## 9. Stop rules

Work SHALL stop when required authority is missing/expired/revoked, a governing dependency is invalid, a Stage attempts to borrow authority, a known applicable requirement remains unmapped, historical closure is silently reinterpreted, Application business semantics leak into Foundation, environment-specific implementation redefines Foundation meaning, or evidence cannot support the claimed transition.

## 10. Proposed supersession rule

If activated, IMP-001 v1.3 SHALL supersede IMP-001 v1.2 prospectively while preserving v1.2 and all historical Stage evidence as immutable predecessors.

Activation SHALL NOT retroactively rewrite the authority, scope, evidence or meaning of any completed Stage/WP.
