## 1. Purpose

TRC-001 v1.4 proposes the traceability extension required by IMP-001 v1.3 without rewriting historical requirement or verification evidence.

It preserves every active and historical trace lineage from TRC-001 v1.3 and adds the prospective Stage mapping required after the Owner-approved Foundation Master Stage Sequence correction.

## 2. Historical Trace Preservation

Historical verification evidence remains bound to the Stage/WP and governing document set under which it was produced.

No historical PASS is relabeled to a new Stage. No future Stage is treated as already verified merely because an earlier component or requirement was previously tested.

## 4. FRS-001 Boundary Rule

VPL-000 and VPL-001 through VPL-008 remain FRS-001 verification plans.

Stages 11 through 17 SHALL NOT be inserted into VPL-000 as hidden FRS requirements.

Post-FRS Foundation capabilities SHALL receive separately governed verification plans during their Stage design. Their eventual plan identifiers, exact requirement mappings, methods, evidence and gates must be assigned prospectively and may not be invented by this trace document before Stage design.

## 6. Foundation Invariant Trace Additions

The successor trace SHALL carry explicit rows or governed references for:

- `ENVIRONMENT_NEUTRALITY_IS_FOUNDATIONAL`;
- `ENVIRONMENT_EVIDENCE_IS_SCOPED`;
- `ZERO_APPLICATION_OPERATION_IS_VALID`;
- `APPLICATIONS_ARE_PLUG_AND_PLAY_CONSUMERS`;
- `NO_APPLICATION_IS_FOUNDATION_PREREQUISITE_BY_DEFAULT`;
- `FOUNDATION_OPERATION_DOES_NOT_CREATE_FINANCIAL_AUTHORITY`;
- `FSA_CORE_OPERATION_DOES_NOT_REQUIRE_EXTERNAL_EGRESS`.

Each invariant must trace to its governing approved sources and later verification evidence. Presence in the trace does not itself create implementation or operational authority.

## 7. Existing-Capability Reconciliation Trace Rule

For every future Stage 7 through Stage 17, each scoped requirement must be classified before new implementation as one of:

- `ALREADY_SATISFIED_BY_ACCEPTED_BASELINE`;
- `PARTIALLY_SATISFIED_REUSE_REQUIRED`;
- `GENUINELY_MISSING`;
- `SUPERSEDED_WITH_TRACE`;
- `OUTSIDE_STAGE_SCOPE`.

Only genuinely missing authorized scope and the missing portion of partially satisfied scope may generate new implementation work.

## 8. Registered Future Specification Rule

A registry-only future Specification subject with no effective body may be traced as `PLANNED_SUBJECT`, but SHALL NOT contribute invented normative requirements.

If its body is later approved and activated, TRC SHALL add the exact requirement identities and mark affected planning rows stale until revalidated.

## 9. Supersession and Staleness

Activation of IMP-001 v1.3, ROADMAP-001 v3.0, or any future Stage Specification SHALL mark affected planning/trace rows stale until the exact successor references are synchronized.

Historical evidence remains historical and shall not be deleted or upgraded by supersession.

## 10. Non-Authority

TRC-001 v1.4 is a documentary trace candidate only. It does not:

- claim any Stage 7 through Stage 17 implementation or verification PASS;
- authorize Stage 6 WP-05 or later work;
- create verification plan bodies for post-FRS Stages;
- activate a Gate, Pipeline, environment, Provider, Application or runtime;
- grant operational, production, external-connectivity or financial authority.
