**Identifier:** FDN-004-NC-001

## Reviewed Clauses

- catalog purpose and source-code model;
- configuration item ownership and source restrictions;
- sensitivity, activation, validation, and compatibility classes;
- universal rules for missing values, protected references, and runtime prohibition of external financial access; and

## Determination

FDN-004 is not materially affected by the AMD-008 / CDA-AMD008-001 alignment work.

The catalog already remains domain-neutral and Foundation-specific. The AMD-008 alignment does not introduce a new configuration source, does not require tier identifiers in the catalog, and does not alter the governed meaning of any declared configuration key.

## Evidence

- FDN-004 remains bound to Foundation-only configuration sources.
- The corrected application-awareness alignment does not change any configuration source, source code, or activation rule in FDN-004.
- No configuration item in FDN-004 depends on the corrected MSA/LSA/CSA hierarchy for its meaning.

## Review Relation

This no-change decision is recorded for completeness in the CDA package and for future activation traceability. It is not an activation record and does not modify FDN-004.

## Result

`NO_CHANGE_CONFIRMED`
