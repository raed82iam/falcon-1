## Contract Rule

Every Contract SHALL define:

- participants;
- authority;
- inputs and outputs;
- preconditions and postconditions;
- invariants;
- errors and rejection;
- compatibility;
- evidence;
- security; and
- acceptance examples.

Serialization, transport, storage, framework, and programming-language choices require ADRs or design decisions and SHALL NOT be inferred from a Contract.

## 1. Purpose

CON-000 is the canonical inventory of Falcon contracts. It records identity, version, status, and primary specification linkage.

## 2. Scope

CON-000 governs:

- contract identity;
- contract name;
- version;
- status;
- primary specification;
- approval lineage;
- contract registry ordering; and
- historical preservation of registry meaning.

## 3. Non-Scope

CON-000 does not:

- define contract semantics;
- create runtime authority;
- approve a contract by listing it;
- activate a contract;
- interpret application meaning; or
- replace the underlying contract documents.

## 4. Normative Requirements

- **CON-000-REQ-001:** Every contract SHALL have one canonical registry entry.
- **CON-000-REQ-002:** Registry status SHALL distinguish Approved, Proposed, Planned, Archived, and Superseded states where applicable.
- **CON-000-REQ-003:** The registry SHALL identify the primary specification for each contract.
- **CON-000-REQ-004:** A registry entry SHALL not be treated as a contract definition.
- **CON-000-REQ-005:** Historical registry states SHALL remain attributable.
- **CON-000-REQ-006:** New contract entries SHALL not create duplicate authority.

## 5. AMD-008 Alignment

The registry proposal SHALL include CON-023 as the Application Contract and Manifest successor and preserve the current foundational contract set without rewriting historical records.

## 6. Failure and Conflict Behavior

If duplicate registry entries, missing primary specification links, or conflicting statuses appear:

- the affected row SHALL be marked invalid for canonical reliance;
- the issue SHALL be visible to the registry review path; and
- no activation SHALL be inferred.

## 7. Invariants

1. Registry entry is not contract definition.
2. One contract, one registry row.
3. Approval by listing is invalid.
4. Historical registry meaning persists.

## 8. Acceptance Evidence

Acceptance requires stable registry rows for current contracts and proposed AMD-008 aligned entries without duplicate canonical ownership.
