**Stage 1 Authority:** Not Granted

## 1. Purpose

This candidate defines the authority that would govern Stage 0C if the complete decision package is separately approved.

## 2. Requested Authority

The requested authority is limited to:

- reconstructing the approved Stage 0A and Stage 0B cases;
- evaluating the exact subjects in STG-0C-SUB-001;
- producing Activation evidence and independent reviews;
- creating active trust material only inside separately admitted custody defined by STG-0C-SEC-001;
- issuing an individual decision for each eligible subject;
- testing restriction, revocation, expiry, and controlled restoration;
- and producing the Foundation Implementation Readiness case.

No subject becomes active through this instrument alone.

## 3. Jurisdiction

Jurisdiction is confined to the Falcon repository, one exact local Windows Foundation build-verification environment, the listed enabling subjects, admitted local tools, and non-financial synthetic verification.

Delegation shall not create jurisdiction.

## 4. Decision Separation

For every subject:

- production, transformation, aggregation, signing, evaluation, completeness, independent review, and Activation shall remain attributable;
- no materially involved party may be the sole Activation Authority;
- the Activation Authority shall rely on exactly one complete, integrity-valid Root Verification Evidence Set;
- validity does not imply Acceptance, and Acceptance does not imply Activation;
- Activation is scoped reliance, not unrestricted trust.

## 5. Prohibitions

This candidate would never authorize general Falcon behavior, Core or Self-Awareness implementation, Stage 1, production, Oracle Cloud, financial connectivity, real financial data, or financial activity.

## 6. Start and Expiry

Stage 0C could start only through explicit Project Owner approval of the complete fourteen-document package.

If approved, authority expires at the earliest of completion, stop, revocation, material change, evidence failure, companion-document supersession, or fourteen calendar days after approval.

## 7. Current Effect

None. This document is a Candidate for review only.
