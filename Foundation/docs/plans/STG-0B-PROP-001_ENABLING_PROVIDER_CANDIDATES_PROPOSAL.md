## 1. Purpose

This proposal defines the next eligible Falcon phase:

```text
Stage 0B — Enabling-Provider Candidates
```

Stage 0B exists to construct and independently verify only the minimum enabling candidates required to establish trustworthy identity, time, cryptographic custody, canonical encoding, evidence, traceability, and bootstrap verification for later Foundation Activation.

This document is a proposal only.

It does not authorize Stage 0B, implementation, tool installation, download, candidate construction, command execution, verification, Activation, production, cloud deployment, financial connectivity, or financial activity.

## 2. Relationship to Prior State

Stage 0A is complete and closed under GOV-049.

Its completion establishes eligibility to propose Stage 0B. It does not establish Stage 0B readiness and does not grant Stage 0B authority.

Foundation remains documentarily closed. Falcon remains unimplemented and inactive.

## 3. Stage Identity

Stage 0B is a bounded enabling-candidate phase.

It is not:

- general Falcon implementation;
- Core behavior implementation;
- Self-Awareness implementation;
- Guardian implementation;
- trading implementation;
- an operational Falcon runtime;
- Provider or Profile Activation;
- a production environment;
- an Oracle Cloud deployment;
- financial-system integration;
- or authority to proceed to Stage 0C.

Stage 0B may create candidates. A candidate is not an active Falcon capability.

## 4. Objective

Stage 0B shall establish whether the minimum enabling candidates can conform to Falcon’s approved Contracts and policies under controlled, reproducible, financially sterile conditions.

Its result is evidence about candidates.

Its result is not trust, Acceptance, Activation, implementation readiness, production readiness, or financial readiness by itself.

## 5. Proposed Candidate Scope

Only the following candidate subjects may be considered:

1. Falcon Canonical Encoding support required by enabling candidates.
2. Trust Object and evidence primitives required for verification.
3. Identifier Provider candidate.
4. Time Provider candidate.
5. Cryptographic Provider Adapter candidate.
6. Secret Provider candidate.
7. Certificate and Identity Provider candidate.
8. Randomness Provider Adapter candidate.
9. Machine-readable traceability expansion support.
10. Bootstrap Pipeline harness.
11. Isolated verification fixtures.

Every authorized candidate shall be named individually in a later Authority Instrument.

An unlisted candidate is prohibited.

## 6. General Behavior Boundary

General Falcon behavior code is prohibited in Stage 0B.

Stage 0B shall not construct:

- Kernel behavior;
- Authority Engine behavior;
- Guardian behavior;
- Lifecycle behavior;
- Service Bus behavior;
- FIL operational messaging;
- Health Monitoring behavior;
- Recovery behavior;
- Event System behavior;
- operational Persistence;
- financial-domain behavior;
- market-data behavior;
- prediction, planning, learning, or trading intelligence;
- execution, order, position, or portfolio behavior;
- Self-Maintenance or Evolution behavior;
- or any other FRS-001 operational behavior.

Enabling support shall remain no broader than required to construct and verify the explicitly authorized candidates.

## 7. Entry Prerequisites

Stage 0B shall not begin unless all applicable prerequisites are separately established:

- an approved Stage 0B Authority Instrument;
- approved applicable Provider and bootstrap Contracts;
- an authorized candidate-verification environment;
- an immutable Bootstrap Execution Context;
- an exact BLD-001 candidate tool scope;
- approved tool sources, versions, integrity checks, and licenses;
- synthetic-only keys, secrets, identities, certificates, time cases, and data;
- an independent evidence path;
- an Evidence Requirement Set fixed before execution;
- exact verification plans and pass criteria;
- independent completeness and promotion boundaries;
- financial-isolation controls;
- secret-custody controls;
- stop, cleanup, challenge, revocation, and expiration rules;
- and a clean repository baseline.

Missing prerequisites shall stop Stage 0B. They shall not be silently waived or inferred from Stage 0A.

## 8. Candidate-Verification Environment

Stage 0B requires a separately authorized candidate-verification environment.

That environment shall:

- remain local Windows for the initial declared scope;
- remain isolated from operational and production environments;
- remain distinct from any future Falcon Environment Profile Activation;
- identify its external bootstrap identity and time;
- use only explicitly admitted tools and materials;
- preserve reproducibility and evidence export;
- prevent financial and cloud connectivity;
- and expire or be closed with Stage 0B authority.

Authorization of a candidate-verification environment does not activate a Falcon operational environment.

Oracle Cloud remains outside Stage 0B.

## 9. Candidate Lifecycle

Every candidate shall remain within the following governed sequence:

```text
Authorized Candidate
→ Constructed Candidate
→ Isolated Verification
→ Evidence Preservation
→ Independent Evaluation
→ Stage 0B Finding
```

Stage 0B shall not extend this sequence to Acceptance or Activation.

Candidate outputs shall be marked as candidate material and shall not satisfy operational dependencies.

No candidate may:

- approve itself;
- evaluate itself as the sole authority;
- declare its own evidence complete;
- activate itself;
- replace an active capability;
- expand its scope;
- or certify a later stage.

## 10. Contract and Adapter Boundary

Candidates shall conform to Falcon Contracts.

External libraries, platform functions, and vendors shall remain behind Falcon-owned Contracts and Adapters.

No external dependency may cross a Falcon layer boundary or become part of Falcon’s governing meaning.

The .NET Base Class Library shall be preferred before an external dependency is proposed.

No Persistence technology, cloud provider, operating system, cryptographic vendor, or external library may become an architectural assumption.

## 11. Synthetic-Only Policy

Stage 0B shall use only governed synthetic or test-only material.

It shall not use:

- production secrets;
- personal secrets;
- reusable private keys;
- cloud credentials;
- financial credentials;
- broker or exchange credentials;
- real identities;
- customer data;
- real market data;
- real orders;
- real positions;
- real portfolios;
- real balances;
- or capital-bearing instructions.

Synthetic material shall be visibly classified, scoped to Stage 0B, non-reusable outside its case, and destroyed or intentionally preserved according to an approved cleanup rule.

## 12. Security and Cryptographic Boundary

Stage 0B security work is candidate verification only.

Cryptographic keys and derived material shall remain separated by governed purpose, environment, identity scope, and protection profile.

Candidate security material shall not:

- enter operational trust;
- certify itself;
- establish an operational root;
- authorize another domain;
- cross into production custody;
- or survive cleanup unless evidence policy explicitly requires safe preservation.

Key usage, domain identifiers, canonical context encoding, Provider behavior, and custody shall follow the approved Falcon security baseline.

## 13. Identity and Time Boundary

Before Provider Activation:

- bootstrap identity remains `BOOTSTRAP_EXTERNAL_ID`;
- bootstrap time remains `BOOTSTRAP_EXTERNAL`;
- candidate-produced identifiers remain candidate outputs;
- candidate time observations remain candidate outputs;
- and neither may be represented as operational Falcon identity or verified Falcon time.

Runtime epochs, uncertainty, provenance, and limitations shall be preserved in evidence.

## 14. Verification Scope

Applicable candidates shall be evaluated through the approved verification baseline, including:

- VPL-BST-003;
- VPL-BST-004;
- VPL-BST-005;
- applicable Contract verification;
- deterministic and repeatability checks;
- negative and fault cases;
- purpose and boundary enforcement;
- evidence integrity;
- dependency and provenance checks;
- and failure containment.

Naming a verification plan in this proposal does not authorize its execution.

Verification execution requires a later exact Authority Instrument and immutable Evidence Requirement Set.

## 15. Evidence Model

Each authorized execution shall preserve:

- Authority Instrument identity;
- Bootstrap Execution Context identity;
- Build Intent;
- candidate identity and version;
- source and dependency provenance;
- tool identity, version, source, and integrity;
- environment identity;
- external bootstrap identity and time;
- inputs and synthetic-material classification;
- commands and actions performed;
- original observations and results;
- failures and stop decisions;
- verification sessions;
- applicable requirements and rules;
- Evaluation Context;
- Derived Evaluations;
- evidence completeness state;
- challenges and resolutions;
- file-change manifest;
- cleanup result;
- and final Stage 0B finding.

Promotion decisions shall reference exactly one Root Verification Evidence Set.

No producer, transformer, aggregator, or signer of evidence may be the sole authority declaring that evidence complete or promotion-ready.

## 16. Independence and Authority

Authority shall be exercised only within declared jurisdiction.

Construction authority, Evaluation Authority, Evidence Completeness Authority, and any later Activation Authority shall remain distinct according to the governing model.

Stage 0B shall provide no Activation Authority.

A successful candidate evaluation is a scoped Claim. It does not compel Acceptance, Activation, reliance, or progression.

## 17. Self-Awareness Boundary

Stage 0B preserves Falcon’s Self-Aware direction without implementing Self-Awareness.

Candidate tooling may observe and report its own bounded verification state only where explicitly required for evidence.

It may not:

- claim consciousness;
- create authority;
- modify Vision or Constitution;
- change governing policies;
- approve or activate itself;
- bypass Guardian;
- conceal uncertainty;
- suppress evidence;
- introduce financial authority;
- or perform autonomous maintenance, development, update, or evolution.

Self-Maintenance and Evolution remain future governed capabilities.

## 18. Financial Isolation

Stage 0B shall remain financially sterile.

No candidate, fixture, harness, test, or evidence process may connect to or affect:

- a broker;
- an exchange;
- a bank;
- a custodian;
- a market-data provider;
- an account;
- a customer;
- an order;
- a position;
- a portfolio;
- a balance;
- or real capital.

Discovery of prohibited financial material shall stop the applicable execution and trigger evidence preservation and Project Owner review.

## 19. Network Boundary

Network use is prohibited unless a later Authority Instrument names:

- the exact destination;
- the exact purpose;
- the exact permitted operation;
- the exact data classification;
- the exact duration;
- and the required evidence.

Cloud access, financial access, telemetry export, remote execution, and undeclared package acquisition remain prohibited.

GitHub publication of approved documentation may be authorized separately. It does not authorize Stage 0B execution.

## 20. Stop Conditions

Stage 0B shall stop when:

- authority is absent, unclear, expired, revoked, exceeded, or materially challenged;
- an unlisted candidate or behavior is required;
- a governing Contract or prerequisite is missing;
- tool or dependency provenance cannot be established;
- evidence cannot be preserved;
- independent evaluation cannot be maintained;
- candidate and operational states become ambiguous;
- synthetic material cannot be proven synthetic and isolated;
- a secret escapes its declared custody;
- financial material or connectivity is discovered;
- cloud access becomes required;
- uncertainty is concealed or treated as certainty;
- a candidate attempts self-approval or self-Activation;
- or Vision, Constitution, Guardian policy, capital protection, or a governing document would be violated.

Failure does not expand authority.

## 21. Exit Criteria

Stage 0B may be assessed complete only when:

- every authorized candidate is accounted for;
- every applicable mandatory verification obligation is evaluated;
- every Root Verification Evidence Set is complete and integrity-valid;
- applicable VPL-BST-003 through VPL-BST-005 findings are preserved;
- candidate limitations and unresolved risks are explicit;
- no candidate is represented as active;
- financial isolation and secret custody are confirmed;
- cleanup is complete or governed preservation is recorded;
- material Challenges are resolved or explicitly block closure;
- repository status before and after is preserved;
- and an independent Stage 0B completion assessment is produced.

Completion of Stage 0B shall not authorize Stage 0C.
