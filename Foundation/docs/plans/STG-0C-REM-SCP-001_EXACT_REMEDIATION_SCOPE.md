## 2. Excluded

Financial domains, market data, trading, brokers, cloud, persistence technology choice, operational messaging, business services, Stage 1 components, and self-modifying behavior are excluded.

## 3. Change Control

Any new subject, external package, vendor SDK, cloud resource, platform expansion, financial input, or architectural boundary requires a new authority decision.
