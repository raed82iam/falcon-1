6. Application failure isolation;
7. Foundation restart/recovery with zero Applications;
8. standalone operation in every environment realization claimed operational.

Stage 17 grants no trading, broker, market-data, capital, investment, financial or Application business authority.

## 6. Dependency interpretation

The order is chosen to minimize rework and prevent reverse dependency:

- Stage 11 is built directly on accepted communication/resource truth.
- Stage 12 uses observable transport and becomes the only generic external-access substrate.
- Stage 13 may consume Stage 12 for research but never requires research to exist or operate.
- Stage 14 supplies canonical accepted artifact publication before Application runtime intake.
- Stage 15 requires Stage 14 and establishes the complete generic runtime hosting surface.
- Stage 16 qualifies that completed runtime on exact environments so later capabilities are not silently added after environment certification.
- Stage 17 is last because it makes the integrated standalone operational-readiness claim.
