# STG-0C-REM-EXIT-001 — Remediation Exit Criteria

**Identifier:** STG-0C-REM-EXIT-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Approval Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Approval Record:** GOV-058  
**Stage 1 Authority:** Not Granted

## 1. Success

Remediation may succeed only when:

- every remaining subject has an exact final disposition;
- every activated subject has an exact Manifest, complete Root Evidence Set, independent review, validity, completeness, restriction, revocation, expiry, and decision;
- no synthetic or bootstrap material entered active custody;
- VPL-BST-006 through VPL-BST-008 pass for the final scope;
- environment, Build, trace, Pipeline, and Gate prerequisites are active in order;
- cloud and financial isolation are proven;
- cleanup is complete;
- and an updated readiness case is produced.

## 2. Failure or Stop

Missing authority, evidence, dependency, custody, identity, time quality, isolation, independent review, or reconstructability prevents success.

## 3. Stage Boundary

Successful remediation may complete Stage 0C. It shall not authorize Stage 1.

Before any Stage 1 discussion or proposal, the Project Owner must receive the previously mandated explicit alert and must grant separate authority.

## 4. Current Effect

No remediation work or Stage 1 work is authorized.
