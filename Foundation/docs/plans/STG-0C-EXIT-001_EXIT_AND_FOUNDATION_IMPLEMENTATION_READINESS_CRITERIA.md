## 1. Purpose

This candidate defines completion, failure, cleanup, preservation, and the boundary before any Stage 1 discussion.

## 2. Completion Criteria

Stage 0C may be assessed complete only when:

- every catalog subject has an explicit final disposition;
- each activated subject has exactly one applicable Root Evidence Set, Validity Assessment, Completeness Decision, independent review, Activation Decision, Manifest, and non-authorities;
- VPL-BST-006 through VPL-BST-008 pass for applicable scope;
- dependency order and authority separation are proven;
- test/bootstrap and active material remain separated;
- restriction, revocation, expiry, and controlled restoration are proven;
- financial and cloud isolation are independently confirmed;
- all material Challenges are resolved;
- cleanup and evidence preservation are complete;
- and the Foundation Implementation Readiness case is complete and integrity-valid.

## 3. Exit States

```text
STAGE_0C_COMPLETE — STAGE 1 MAY BE DISCUSSED AND PROPOSED
STAGE_0C_INCOMPLETE — REMEDIATION REQUIRES AUTHORITY
STAGE_0C_FAILED — NO SUBJECT MAY ADVANCE
STAGE_0C_STOPPED — AUTHORITY REVIEW REQUIRED
```

## 4. Readiness Case

The readiness case shall state what is active, restricted, rejected, unresolved, and explicitly unauthorized. It may recommend whether a Stage 1 proposal should be prepared.

It shall not authorize Stage 1.

## 5. Mandatory Owner Alert Before Stage 1

Before any Stage 1 proposal, preparation, or work, the Project Owner shall receive a clear notice that:

- Stage 0C is complete and what its evidence proves;
- Stage 1 is a new implementation phase;
- the exact requested Stage 1 scope, risks, and non-authorities;
- a separate explicit approval is required;
- and no silence, prior approval, readiness finding, or technical success grants progression.

No Stage 1 document package shall be prepared and no Stage 1 action shall begin before that alert and separate Project Owner authorization.

## 6. Current Effect

Stage 0C is not started. Stage 1 remains prohibited.
