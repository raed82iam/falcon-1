- its Prime Objective is Protect Capital, Manage Capital, then Grow Capital;
- capital protection prevails over opportunity;
- intelligence informs but does not own execution authority;

## 8. Self-Awareness Assessment

The Foundation concept supports governed Self-Awareness:

- observe internal and external state;
- assess quality, confidence, limits, health, drift, and uncertainty;
- diagnose bounded faults;
- propose maintenance, repair, update, and evolution;
- prepare candidates under explicit authority;
- require independent verification;
- require competent acceptance and exact Activation;
- preserve rollback;
- monitor post-change fitness; and
- restrict itself when evidence is missing or risk is unacceptable.

Self-Awareness cannot:

- change Vision or Constitution;
- create jurisdiction;
- expand its authority;
- accept or activate its own changes;
- bypass Guardian or independent verification;
- conceal uncertainty;
- restore a compromised subject automatically;
- connect to financial systems without separate authority; or
- treat technical capability as permission.

**Assessment:** Governing concept is `READY`; implementation is `NOT AUTHORIZED`.
