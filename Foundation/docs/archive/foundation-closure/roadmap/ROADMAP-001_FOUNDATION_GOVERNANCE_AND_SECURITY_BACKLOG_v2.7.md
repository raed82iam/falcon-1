- does not authorize production or financial activity.

## 16. Linux Roadmap

- a separate Activation Decision.

Linux execution is not required to delay the first Windows Foundation case unless the governing obligation explicitly requires cross-platform evidence.

## 17. Oracle Cloud Roadmap

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure is the intended first future cloud direction.

Before OCI admission Falcon requires a separate governed Environment Profile covering:

- tenancy, compartment, region, availability, and data residency;
- workload and administrative identity;
- network, private endpoints, egress, ingress, and DNS;
- compute, image, virtualization, container, and orchestration boundaries;
- storage, backup, snapshot, replication, deletion, and recovery;
- cryptographic, secret, certificate, identity, and randomness Adapters;
- time, evidence, logging, retention, and independent access;
- outage, partition, throttling, quota, billing, and control-plane failure;
- shared-responsibility and privileged-provider threats;
- migration, replacement, portability, and exit;
- cleanup and residual-resource evidence; and
- a separate exact OCI Activation Decision.

OCI is not provisioned, admitted, active, or required for the initial Windows Foundation.

## 18. Self-Awareness, Maintenance, and Evolution Roadmap

Falcon’s future Self-Maintenance and Evolution System shall support:

- continuous observation;
- health, drift, dependency, evidence, threat, and lifecycle diagnosis;
- bounded repair and update proposals;
- sandboxed candidate construction;
- independent verification;
- competent acceptance and exact Activation;
- rollback;
- post-change monitoring; and
- learned improvement within governing limits.

It shall never:

- change the Vision or Constitution;
- expand its own jurisdiction or authority;
- accept, activate, or promote its own changes;
- bypass Guardian or independent verification;
- conceal uncertainty;
- reactivate a failed or compromised subject automatically;
- use production or financial authority to test itself; or
- alter capital-bearing behavior without separate future governance and authority.

Self-Awareness is a governed capability, not sovereign authority.

## 20. Documentation Control Rules

1. Vision remains the highest project authority.
2. Constitution remains subordinate to Vision and superior to all lower documents.
3. Contracts remain within the approved document-authority order.
4. ADRs record consequential solution decisions.
5. Specifications define required behavior, properties, constraints, and acceptance outcomes.
6. Catalogs define governed values.
7. Designs define implementation structure without granting implementation authority.
8. Plans define governed work and verification sequencing.
9. Roadmap status requires exact approval evidence.
10. Supersession preserves historical and inherited requirement meaning.
11. Approval of a document does not activate the subject it describes unless the decision explicitly and competently does so.
12. No Roadmap entry creates jurisdiction, authority, implementation permission, operational trust, or financial capability.
