Because the freeze identity was corrected, the Red-Team specifically re-attacked the final research/egress interpretation surfaced by the final index.

Blocked. Final controlling rule explicitly forbids Trading MSA direct Internet access and routes research acquisition through bounded non-Live FSTSimA/research-sandbox behavior when Foundation research egress exists and is authorized.

### Attack B — FSA inherits Application research permission

Blocked. FSA direct Internet remains forbidden. Foundation owns any future separate FSA-relevant research mechanism.

### Attack C — FSTSimA research route becomes operational market-data feed

Blocked. Research Evidence Package is non-operational and non-authoritative; FSAPMA remains the sole operational provider-data gateway.

### Attack D — research sandbox places broker orders

Blocked. Research Sandbox has no broker execution authority and no direct path to trusted runtime deployment.

### Attack E — malicious webpage instructions override Falcon rules

Blocked. Retrieved content is data, not instruction authority; quarantine, typed tools, no raw credentials and no direct side effects apply.

### Attack F — downloaded strategy/model is installed directly

Blocked. External artifacts enter candidate/provenance/validation lifecycle only.

### Attack G — same vendor provides market data and brokerage, causing route/credential merge

Blocked. Service roles, egress identities and credential references remain distinct even when vendor identity is the same.

### Attack H — Paper credentials silently fall back to Live

Blocked. Paper/Live identity/domain/account/credential separation is explicit and no permissive fallback exists.
